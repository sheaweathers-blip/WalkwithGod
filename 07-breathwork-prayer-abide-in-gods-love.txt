Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Abide in God's Love
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 6-8 minutes
Scripture: John 15:9, "As the Father has loved me, so have I loved you. Abide in my love."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Abide in God's Love.

Settle into a comfortable position. Let your spine be supported. Let your hands rest. Let your breathing be natural.

If your body needs to shift, shift gently. If you need to return to normal breathing, do that freely.

This is not a performance.
This is an invitation to remain with Jesus.

Our Scripture today is John 15:9:
"As the Father has loved me, so have I loved you. Abide in my love."

Take a slow breath in.
And breathe out.

Again, breathe in.
And breathe out.

Let these words become the rhythm:

Inhale:
"God, You love me."

Exhale:
"Help me abide."

Inhale:
"God, You love me."

Exhale:
"Help me abide."

To abide means to remain.
To stay.
To make your home in His love.

You do not have to earn your way into this moment.
You are already being invited to remain.

Inhale:
"I receive Your love."

Exhale:
"I release my striving."

Inhale:
"I receive Your love."

Exhale:
"I release my striving."

Notice any place where you feel like you have been trying to prove yourself.
To God.
To people.
To yourself.

Hold that place before Jesus.

Pause.

Hear the invitation again:
"Abide in my love."

Inhale:
"I am held by Christ."

Exhale:
"I can stop striving."

Inhale:
"I am held by Christ."

Exhale:
"I can stop striving."

Now ask:
"Jesus, what would it look like to remain in Your love today?"

Pause.

Maybe abiding looks like patience.
Maybe it looks like confession.
Maybe it looks like receiving grace.
Maybe it looks like loving someone from a steadier place.
Maybe it looks like rest.

Take three quiet breaths with God.

Pause.

Lord Jesus, thank You for loving me before I could earn anything. Teach me to remain in Your love today. Let my words, choices, movement, rest, and relationships grow from being loved by You. Free me from striving and form Your fruit in me.

Help me abide.
Help me walk with You.

Amen.
