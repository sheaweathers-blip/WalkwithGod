Walk With God Abide Script
Title: New Morning Mercies
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 6-8 minutes
Scripture: Lamentations 3:22-23, "The steadfast love of the Lord never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning; great is your faithfulness."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called New Morning Mercies.

This practice is for the beginning of a new day, or any moment when you need to begin again with God.

Find a comfortable seated position. Let your feet rest on the floor if you are sitting. Let your hands soften. Let your jaw unclench. Let your shoulders lower.

If any breath pattern feels uncomfortable, return to normal breathing and simply listen.

Our Scripture today is Lamentations 3:22-23:
"The steadfast love of the Lord never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning; great is your faithfulness."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Again, breathe in.
And breathe out.

You are not entering this day alone.
You are not entering this day empty-handed.
God's mercy is already here.

As you inhale, quietly pray:
"Your mercies are new."

As you exhale, pray:
"I begin again with You."

Inhale:
"Your mercies are new."

Exhale:
"I begin again with You."

Continue that rhythm.

Let the inhale remind you that God's mercy is given.
Let the exhale remind you that yesterday does not get the final word.

If your mind starts listing everything waiting for you today, gently return to the prayer.

Inhale:
"Your mercies are new."

Exhale:
"I begin again with You."

Now bring before God anything from yesterday that still feels heavy.
A mistake.
A regret.
A disappointment.
A word you wish you could take back.
A task left undone.

You do not have to drag yesterday into today without God.

Inhale:
"I receive God's mercy."

Exhale:
"I release yesterday."

Inhale:
"I receive God's mercy."

Exhale:
"I release yesterday."

Pause.

Now bring before God the day ahead.
The people you will see.
The work you will do.
The choices you will make.
The body you will care for.
The thoughts you will need to guard.

Ask Him to walk with you through it.

Inhale:
"Great is Your faithfulness."

Exhale:
"Lead me today."

Inhale:
"Great is Your faithfulness."

Exhale:
"Lead me today."

Now take three natural breaths in silence.

Pause.

Lord, thank You that Your steadfast love does not run out. Thank You that Your mercy is not old, stale, or far away. It is new this morning because You are faithful.

Help me begin this day with You.
Help me receive mercy instead of shame.
Help me offer mercy instead of irritation.
Help me care for the body You created and walk into this day with a steady heart.

May my breathing, my words, my work, and my relationships become part of my walk with You.

Amen.
