# Canva Build Plan: Abide Breath Prayer Video 1

## Video

Title: Be Still and Breathe  
Category: Abide Breath Prayer  
Recommended Canva format: Mobile Video, 1080 x 1920  
Estimated length: 5 to 7 minutes  
Main Scripture: Psalm 46:10, "Be still, and know that I am God."

## Files To Upload To Canva

- Logo: `assets/walk-with-god-logo.png`
- Mobile title card: `premium-scripts/logo-intro/logo-intro-mobile-title-card.png`
- Mobile ending card: `premium-scripts/logo-intro/logo-intro-mobile-end-card.png`
- Main voice audio: `premium-media/breathwork-day-1.wav`
- Recommended background music: `assets/music/living-waters.mp3`
- Alternate music: `assets/music/nature-breath.mp3`
- Brighter alternate music: `assets/music/sunlit-dream.mp3`

## Overall Feel

Calm, warm, peaceful, and devotional. The video should feel like a guided moment with God, not a workout class or a busy meditation app.

Use nature footage that feels slow and breathable:

- Sunlight through trees
- Gentle water
- Meadow or walking path
- Soft clouds
- Quiet lake
- Green hills
- Slow forest trail

Avoid fast cuts, dramatic worship-stage lighting, intense fitness visuals, or anything too dark.

## Canva Design Settings

Canvas size: Mobile Video, 1080 x 1920  
Font suggestion: Playfair Display or Libre Baskerville for titles, Lato or Open Sans for body text  
Main text color: dark forest green  
Accent color: soft gold  
Text background: optional translucent cream box at 65% to 75% opacity  
Animation style: fade, breathe, or soft rise only  
Transition style: dissolve or fade only  

Keep text short. Let the audio lead.

## Audio Settings

Main voice audio:

- Set `breathwork-day-1.wav` to 100%
- Keep it clear and easy to understand

Background music:

- Use `living-waters.mp3` first
- Start around 8% to 12% volume
- Lower to 6% to 8% if it competes with the voice
- Fade music in during the intro
- Fade music out during the final 5 seconds

If the music feels too busy, use `nature-breath.mp3` instead.

## Simple Canva Steps

1. Open Canva.
2. Choose Mobile Video, 1080 x 1920.
3. Upload the logo cards, breathwork audio, and one music track.
4. Add `logo-intro-mobile-title-card.png` as the first page.
5. Add a peaceful nature video background for each main scene.
6. Add `breathwork-day-1.wav` as the main audio.
7. Add `living-waters.mp3` under the full video at low volume.
8. Add the text overlays from the timeline.
9. Use fade transitions only.
10. Add `logo-intro-mobile-end-card.png` as the final page.
11. Play the full video once while listening for voice clarity.
12. Export as MP4.

- The voice is easy to hear.
- The music is soft and does not compete with the prayer.
- Text is readable on a phone.
- No scene feels visually busy.
- The logo appears clearly at the beginning and end.
- The Scripture is shown long enough to read.
- The video feels peaceful, not rushed.
