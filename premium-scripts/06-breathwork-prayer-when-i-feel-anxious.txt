Walk With God Abide Script
Title: When I Feel Anxious
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 7-9 minutes
Scripture: Philippians 4:6-7, "Do not be anxious about anything..."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called When I Feel Anxious.

Find a steady, comfortable position. Let your feet touch the floor if you are seated. Let your hands rest somewhere easy. Let your breath be normal for a moment.

If breathing exercises ever make you feel dizzy, lightheaded, or uncomfortable, stop the pattern and breathe normally. You are not failing. You are caring for the body God gave you.

Our Scripture today is Philippians 4:6-7:
"Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to God."

Anxiety can make everything feel urgent.
So we are going to slow down gently.

Take a slow breath in through your nose if you can.
And breathe out softly.

Again, breathe in.
And breathe out.

For the next few moments, breathe in for four counts and breathe out for six counts.

Inhale:
One.
Two.
Three.
Four.

Exhale:
One.
Two.
Three.
Four.
Five.
Six.

Again.

Inhale:
One.
Two.
Three.
Four.

Exhale:
One.
Two.
Three.
Four.
Five.
Six.

Let the longer exhale become a quiet release.

Now add these words.

Inhale:
"God, You are here."

Exhale:
"I bring this to You."

Inhale:
"God, You are here."

Exhale:
"I bring this to You."

If your thoughts race, that is okay.
You can return gently.
No shame.
No pressure.

Now name one anxious thought before God.
You do not have to explain it perfectly.
You can simply say:
"Lord, this is what I am carrying."

Pause.

Scripture does not tell us to pretend we are not anxious.
It invites us to bring everything to God in prayer.

Inhale:
"I am not alone."

Exhale:
"God guards my heart."

Inhale:
"I am not alone."

Exhale:
"God guards my mind."

Now take three slow breaths.

Pause.

Ask God for the next faithful step.
Not the whole plan.
Just the next faithful step.

Maybe the next step is rest.
Maybe it is asking for help.
Maybe it is telling the truth.
Maybe it is waiting.
Maybe it is doing one small thing instead of everything at once.

Inhale:
"Give me wisdom."

Exhale:
"Lead my next step."

Inhale:
"Give me wisdom."

Exhale:
"Lead my next step."

Lord, I bring You what feels anxious, tangled, and heavy. Thank You that I do not have to carry it alone. Guard my heart and mind in Christ Jesus. Give me wisdom for what is mine to do, courage to ask for help when I need it, and trust for what belongs in Your hands.

Help me walk with You gently from here.

Amen.
