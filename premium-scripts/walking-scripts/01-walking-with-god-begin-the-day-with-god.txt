Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Begin the Day With God
Category: Walking With God
Estimated Length: 15 minutes
Pace: Easy pace
Scripture: Psalm 143:8

Recording note: This is written word-for-word for a guided audio walk. Leave the silence cues in the recording as quiet space with music or natural sound. Keep the user's safety in mind; they should stay aware of surroundings, traffic, weather, and their body.

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's guided walk is called Begin the Day With God.

Before we begin, choose a safe place to walk. Stay aware of your surroundings. Keep your eyes open. Adjust your pace whenever you need to. If walking is not available to you today, you can do this seated, stretching, standing outside, or moving gently in another safe way.

This is not a performance. This is time with God. We are caring for the body He created, and we are making room for prayer, reflection, and conversation with Him.

Our Scripture today is Psalm 143:8:
"Let the morning bring me word of your unfailing love, for I have put my trust in you. Show me the way I should go, for to you I entrust my life."

Begin walking slowly.

Notice your first few steps.
Notice your breath.
Notice the ground beneath you.
Let your shoulders soften.
Let your jaw relax.
Let your hands be easy.

You are not just exercising.
You are making space to walk with God.

For the next few breaths, let these words become a simple prayer.

Inhale:
"Lord, I receive Your love."

Exhale:
"Show me the way to go."

Again.

Inhale:
"Lord, I receive Your love."

Exhale:
"Show me the way to go."

[Pause for 45 seconds of quiet walking.]

Now bring your attention back to the Scripture:
"Let the morning bring me word of your unfailing love, for I have put my trust in you. Show me the way I should go, for to you I entrust my life."

Let the words settle slowly. You do not need to force an answer. You do not need to make this moment impressive. Just keep walking with God.

Name the day before God. Tell Him what is ahead, what feels heavy, and what you are hoping
for.

Speak with God honestly as you walk. Use plain words. Tell Him what is true. If your mind wanders, gently return to the breath prayer.

Inhale:
"Lord, I receive Your love."

Exhale:
"Show me the way to go."

[Pause for 90 seconds of quiet walking.]

Now continue at a comfortable pace.

Ask God to show you one faithful step for today. Not the whole plan. Just the next step.

If a word, memory, person, or conviction comes to mind, hold it before God without rushing to solve it. Ask Him, "Lord, what are You inviting me to notice?"

[Pause for 90 seconds of quiet walking.]

Now take a slightly deeper breath if that feels comfortable.

Look around you.
Notice the sky.
Notice the path.
Notice one sign of life near you.

God is present here, in this ordinary place.

Think of one person you may see, message, serve, or remember today. Pray for them as you
walk.

Let this part of the walk become prayer. You can pray silently, whisper, or simply hold the person or situation before God.

[Pause for 2 minutes of quiet walking.]

As we move toward the final part of this walk, slow your pace just a little.

Return again to the breath prayer.

Inhale:
"Lord, I receive Your love."

Exhale:
"Show me the way to go."

Again.

Inhale:
"Lord, I receive Your love."

Exhale:
"Show me the way to go."

[Pause for 60 seconds of quiet walking.]

Now ask God one question:
"What is one faithful step I can take from this walk?"

Not ten steps.
Not a perfect plan.
One faithful step.

Maybe it is a prayer to continue.
Maybe it is a message to send.
Maybe it is forgiveness to begin.
Maybe it is rest to receive.
Maybe it is courage, confession, patience, gratitude, or obedience in something small.

[Pause for 90 seconds of quiet walking.]

Begin to slow down.

Notice your breath again.
Notice your body.
Notice that God has been with you through every step.

Pray this with me:

Lord, let this day begin with You. Remind me of Your unfailing love. Teach me to trust You with what I cannot control. Show me the way I should go. Walk with me today.

Thank You for this body.
Thank You for this breath.
Thank You for this path.
Help me keep walking with You in ordinary life.

In the name of Jesus,
Amen.
