Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Be Still and Breathe
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 5-7 minutes
Scripture: Psalm 46:10, "Be still, and know that I am God."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Be Still and Breathe.

Before we begin, find a comfortable seated position. Let your shoulders soften. Let your hands rest open, palms up if that feels natural.

If at any point you feel dizzy, uncomfortable, or short of breath, return to normal breathing.

Our Scripture today is Psalm 46:10:
"Be still, and know that I am God."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Again, breathe in.
And breathe out.

Continue to breathe slowly, inhaling for 4 seconds and exhaling for 4 seconds

You do not have to perform for God here.
You do not have to have the perfect words.
You are simply making space to be with Him.

As you inhale, quietly pray:
"Be still."

As you exhale, pray:
"You are God."

Inhale:
"Be still."

Exhale:
"You are God."

Continue that rhythm.

Let your body become a place of prayer.
Let your breath slow your thoughts.
Let this moment remind you that God is near.

If your mind wanders, gently return to the words:
"Be still."
"You are God."

Now bring before God anything you have been carrying today.
A worry.
A responsibility.
A fear.
A decision.
A person you love.

You do not have to solve it in this moment.
You can place it before Him.

Inhale:
"I receive God's peace."

Exhale:
"I release my hurry."

Inhale:
"I receive Your presence."

Exhale:
"I release control."

Now take three natural breaths in silence.

Pause.

Lord, thank You for meeting me in stillness.
Thank You that I am not alone.
Teach me to care for the body You created, quiet my anxious thoughts, and return to You throughout this day.

Help me remember:
You are God.
You are near.
You are faithful.

Amen.
