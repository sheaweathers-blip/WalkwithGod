Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Receive God's Peace
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 6-8 minutes
Scripture: John 14:27, "Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to you."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Receive God's Peace.

Before we begin, find a comfortable position. You may sit, stand, or lie down. Let your shoulders soften. Let your jaw relax. Let your hands rest open if that feels natural.

If at any point you feel dizzy, uncomfortable, or short of breath, return to normal breathing.

This practice is not about controlling your body or achieving a spiritual state. It is simply a way to slow down, pray, and receive the nearness of God.

Our Scripture today is John 14:27:
"Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to you."

Take a natural breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Again, breathe in.
And breathe out.

Let the next few breaths become a quiet welcome.

Inhale:
"Jesus, You are near."

Exhale:
"I receive God's peace."

Inhale:
"Jesus, You are near."

Exhale:
"I receive God's peace."

Continue that rhythm.

You do not have to force peace.
You do not have to manufacture calm.
Jesus gives peace as a gift.

As you breathe, notice any place in your body that feels tense.
Your forehead.
Your shoulders.
Your chest.
Your stomach.
Your hands.

Do not judge what you notice.
Simply bring it into prayer.

Inhale slowly:
"Your peace is a gift."

Exhale slowly:
"I do not have to strive."

Inhale:
"Your peace is a gift."

Exhale:
"I do not have to strive."

Now bring one concern before the Lord.
Not all of them.
Just one.

Name it quietly.

Pause.

Jesus does not shame you for needing peace.
He meets His people with mercy.

Inhale:
"I give this to You."

Exhale:
"Hold me in Your peace."

Inhale:
"I give this to You."

Exhale:
"Hold me in Your peace."

Take three quiet breaths without words.

Pause.

Lord Jesus, thank You that Your peace is not fragile. Thank You that Your peace is not dependent on perfect circumstances. Teach me to receive what You freely give. Help me walk into the rest of this day with a quieter heart, a steadier mind, and a deeper trust in Your presence.

You are near.
You are faithful.
Your peace is enough for this next step.

Amen.
