Title: How to Read Scripture With God
Category: Bible Study
Estimated Length: 15-20 minutes
Scripture: 2 Timothy 3:16-17

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's Bible study session is called How to Read Scripture With God.

Our Scripture is 2 Timothy 3:16-17:
"All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness, so that the servant of God may be thoroughly equipped for every good work."

Before we study, take a moment to pray:

Lord, open my heart to Your Word.
Help me come with humility, attention, and obedience.
Teach me not only to understand Scripture, but to be shaped by it.
Amen.

Today we are learning a simple way to read Scripture with God.

There are four movements:

First: Read slowly.
Second: Notice what is there.
Third: Listen for what God reveals.
Fourth: Respond with one faithful step.

Let's begin with reading slowly.

Many times we rush Scripture because we are looking for a quick answer. But the Bible is not just information. It is formation. God uses His Word to shape us.

So when you read, slow down.
Read the passage more than once.
Notice repeated words.
Notice commands.
Notice promises.
Notice warnings.
Notice what the passage reveals about God.

Second: Notice what is there.

Ask:
Who is speaking?
Who is listening?
What is happening?
What comes before and after this passage?
Is this a command, promise, prayer, warning, story, or teaching?

This helps us avoid making the passage mean only what we want it to mean.

Third: Listen for what God reveals.

A helpful question is:
"What does this passage show me about God?"

Not only:
"What does this mean for me?"

But first:
Who is God showing Himself to be?

Is He holy?
Merciful?
Patient?
Just?
Near?
Faithful?
Powerful?
Compassionate?

When we see God more clearly, we respond more faithfully.

Fourth: Respond with one faithful step.

James 1:22 reminds us not to only listen to the Word, but to do what it says.

So after reading, ask:
Is there something to believe?
Something to confess?
Something to obey?
Someone to forgive?
Someone to serve?
Something to surrender?
Something to remember?

Do not overwhelm yourself with ten changes.
Choose one faithful response.

Now let's return to 2 Timothy 3:16-17.

This passage teaches us that Scripture is God-breathed.
That means Scripture is not merely human wisdom. It carries God's authority, truth, and life.

It is useful for teaching.
God's Word shows us what is true.

It is useful for rebuking.
God's Word lovingly exposes what is wrong.

It is useful for correcting.
God's Word does not only confront us. It redirects us.

It is useful for training in righteousness.
God's Word forms us over time.

And the purpose is beautiful:
That the servant of God may be equipped for every good work.

So today's reflection question is:

Where do I need Scripture to teach, correct, or train me right now?

And today's practice is:

Choose one short passage of Scripture.
Read it three times.
Write down:
One thing it reveals about God.
One thing it reveals about you.
One faithful step you can take today.

Let's close in prayer.

Lord, thank You for Your Word.
Forgive me for the times I rush it, ignore it, or use it only for quick answers.
Teach me to love Scripture because it leads me closer to You.
Let Your Word correct me without shame, guide me without confusion, and equip me for the good works You have prepared.

Shape my heart, my thoughts, my words, and my actions.

Amen.
