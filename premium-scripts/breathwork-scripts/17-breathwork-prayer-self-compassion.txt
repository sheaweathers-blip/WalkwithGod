Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Self-Compassion
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 5-7 minutes
Scripture: Psalm 103:13-14, "As a father shows compassion to his children, so the Lord shows compassion to those who fear him. For he knows our frame; he remembers that we are dust."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Self-Compassion.

Place one hand over your heart if that feels comfortable. Let your shoulders soften. Return to normal breathing if anything feels uncomfortable.

This is not self-pity.
This is learning to receive the compassion God gives.

Our Scripture today is Psalm 103:13-14:
"The Lord shows compassion... for he knows our frame."

Take a gentle breath in.
And slowly breathe out.

Inhale:
"God is compassionate."

Exhale:
"He remembers I am human."

Inhale:
"God is compassionate."

Exhale:
"He remembers I am human."

Notice where you have been harsh with yourself.
Maybe about your body.
Your pace.
Your emotions.
Your mistakes.
Your limits.

Bring that harshness into God's light.

Inhale:
"I receive God's compassion."

Exhale:
"I release harsh judgment."

Inhale:
"I receive God's compassion."

Exhale:
"I release harsh judgment."

You are not loved because you are perfect.
You are loved because God is merciful.

Let that truth settle.

Pause.

Take three quiet breaths.

Father, help me receive Your compassion. Teach me to speak to myself with truth and kindness. Heal the places where shame has sounded louder than Your mercy. Help me care for this body, mind, and soul as gifts You made.

You remember my frame.
You meet me with compassion.

Amen.
