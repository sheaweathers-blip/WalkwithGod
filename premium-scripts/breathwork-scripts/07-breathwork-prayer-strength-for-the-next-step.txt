Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Strength for the Next Step
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 6-8 minutes
Scripture: Isaiah 40:31, "But they who wait for the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings like eagles; they shall run and not be weary; they shall walk and not faint."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Strength for the Next Step.

This practice is for the moment when you feel tired, stretched thin, or unsure how to keep going. You do not need strength for the entire road right now. You can ask God for strength for the next faithful step.

Find a comfortable position. Sit tall without forcing your posture. Let your chest be open. Let your hands rest softly.

If any breath pattern feels uncomfortable, release it and breathe normally.

Our Scripture today is Isaiah 40:31:
"But they who wait for the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings like eagles; they shall run and not be weary; they shall walk and not faint."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Again, breathe in.
And breathe out.

Notice where your body feels tired.
Notice where your mind feels crowded.
Notice where your heart feels pressured.

You do not have to pretend with God.
He meets you honestly.

As you inhale, quietly pray:
"I wait on You."

As you exhale, pray:
"Renew my strength."

Inhale:
"I wait on You."

Exhale:
"Renew my strength."

Continue that rhythm.

Waiting on the Lord is not giving up.
It is turning toward the One who does not grow weary.
It is letting your strength come from Him instead of from pressure, fear, or hurry.

Inhale:
"I wait on You."

Exhale:
"Renew my strength."

Now bring one next step before God.
Not the whole month.
Not the whole problem.
Just one next step.

Maybe it is a conversation.
A chore.
A boundary.
A walk.
A prayer.
A moment of obedience.
A decision to rest.

Hold that next step before Him.

Inhale:
"You are with me."

Exhale:
"I can take this step."

Inhale:
"You are with me."

Exhale:
"I can take this step."

Pause.

Now soften your hands.
Soften your forehead.
Let your breath become steady and simple.

Inhale:
"Not by my striving."

Exhale:
"But by Your strength."

Inhale:
"Not by my striving."

Exhale:
"But by Your strength."

Now take three quiet breaths in silence.

Pause.

Lord, thank You that You do not shame me for being tired. Thank You that You renew strength for those who wait on You.

Help me care for the body You created with wisdom and patience.
Help me move today without rushing ahead of You.
Help me walk, not faint.
Help me run, not grow weary.
Help me take the next faithful step with You.

Amen.
