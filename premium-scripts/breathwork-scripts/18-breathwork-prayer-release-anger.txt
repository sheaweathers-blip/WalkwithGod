Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Release Anger
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 5-7 minutes
Scripture: James 1:19-20, "Let every person be quick to hear, slow to speak, slow to anger; for the anger of man does not produce the righteousness of God."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Release Anger.

Find a steady posture. Let your feet feel grounded. If your body feels too activated for breathwork, breathe normally and listen gently.

This practice is not about denying anger.
It is about bringing anger under God's care before it controls your words or actions.

Our Scripture today is James 1:19-20:
"Be quick to hear, slow to speak, slow to anger."

Take a breath in.
And slowly breathe out.

Let the exhale be a little longer than the inhale.

Inhale:
"Lord, slow me down."

Exhale:
"Guard my words."

Inhale:
"Lord, slow me down."

Exhale:
"Guard my words."

Now notice the anger.
Where do you feel it?
In your chest?
Your jaw?
Your stomach?
Your hands?

Do not feed it.
Do not shame it.
Bring it to God.

Inhale:
"Help me listen."

Exhale:
"Make me slow to speak."

Inhale:
"Help me listen."

Exhale:
"Make me slow to speak."

Ask God what is underneath the anger.
Hurt?
Fear?
Injustice?
Exhaustion?
Pride?

Pause.

Lord, my anger needs Your wisdom. Keep me from sinning with my words, silence, assumptions, or reactions. Help me listen well, speak truthfully, pursue what is right, and release what is not mine to carry.

Make me quick to hear.
Slow to speak.
Slow to anger.

Amen.
