Walk With God Abide Script
Title: God's Peace
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 5-7 minutes
Scripture: Philippians 4:7, "The peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called God's Peace.

Find a comfortable posture. Let your shoulders soften. Let your breath slow naturally. If the breath pattern does not feel good, return to normal breathing.

Our Scripture today is Philippians 4:7:
"The peace of God... will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Inhale:
"Peace of God."

Exhale:
"Guard my heart."

Inhale:
"Peace of God."

Exhale:
"Guard my mind."

Again.

Inhale:
"Peace of God."

Exhale:
"Guard my heart."

Inhale:
"Peace of God."

Exhale:
"Guard my mind."

Now notice what feels unguarded.
Your thoughts.
Your emotions.
Your sleep.
Your relationships.
Your future.
Your body.

Bring that place to Christ.

Inhale:
"Christ Jesus, hold me."

Exhale:
"Let Your peace stand guard."

Inhale:
"Christ Jesus, hold me."

Exhale:
"Let Your peace stand guard."

This peace may surpass understanding.
You may not be able to explain it.
You may simply receive it.

Take three quiet breaths.

Pause.

Lord, let Your peace guard my heart and mind in Christ Jesus. Stand watch over what feels exposed in me. Teach me to receive peace even before every question is answered.

Your peace is enough for this moment.

Amen.
