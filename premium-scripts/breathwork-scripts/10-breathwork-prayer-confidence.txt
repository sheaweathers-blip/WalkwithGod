Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Confidence
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 5-7 minutes
Scripture: Joshua 1:9, "Be strong and courageous. Do not be frightened, and do not be dismayed, for the Lord your God is with you wherever you go."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Confidence.

Settle into a steady posture. Let your spine rise gently. Let your shoulders relax. If you feel uncomfortable or lightheaded, return to normal breathing.

Biblical confidence is not self-reliance. It is courage rooted in God's presence.

Our Scripture today is Joshua 1:9:
"Be strong and courageous... for the Lord your God is with you wherever you go."

Take a slow breath in.
And slowly breathe out.

We will breathe evenly.
Inhale for five.
Exhale for five.

Inhale:
"God is with me."

Exhale:
"I can be courageous."

Inhale:
"God is with me."

Exhale:
"I can be courageous."

Notice where fear has been making you small.
Maybe in your voice.
Maybe in your obedience.
Maybe in a conversation.
Maybe in a new responsibility.

You do not have to shame the fear.
Just bring it to God.

Inhale:
"You are with me."

Exhale:
"I will take the next step."

Inhale:
"You are with me."

Exhale:
"I will take the next step."

Let your body hear the truth:
You are not alone.
You are not abandoned.
You are not walking in your own strength.

Take three steady breaths.

Pause.

Lord, make me strong and courageous, not because I feel ready, but because You are with me. Help me stop shrinking back from obedience. Give me humility without insecurity, courage without pride, and confidence that depends on Your presence. Lead me into the next faithful thing.

You are with me wherever I go.

Amen.
