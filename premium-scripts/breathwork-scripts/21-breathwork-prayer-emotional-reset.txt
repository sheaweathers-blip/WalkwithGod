Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Emotional Reset
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 8-10 minutes
Scripture: Psalm 62:8, "Trust in him at all times, O people; pour out your heart before him; God is a refuge for us."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Emotional Reset.

Find a comfortable, supported position. Let yourself arrive slowly. If breathing deeply feels uncomfortable, breathe naturally and listen.

This is a place to bring your whole heart to God.
Not the polished version.
The real one.

Our Scripture today is Psalm 62:8:
"Pour out your heart before him; God is a refuge for us."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Inhale:
"God is my refuge."

Exhale:
"I can be honest here."

Inhale:
"God is my refuge."

Exhale:
"I can be honest here."

Now notice what emotions are present.
Anxiety.
Sadness.
Anger.
Gratitude.
Numbness.
Confusion.
Hope.

You do not have to sort them perfectly.
Just notice.

Inhale:
"This is what I feel."

Exhale:
"God, You are my refuge."

Inhale:
"This is what I feel."

Exhale:
"God, You are my refuge."

Now quietly pour out your heart.
Use words if they come.
Use silence if they do not.

Pause.

Let your breath become gentle again.

Inhale:
"You receive my heart."

Exhale:
"You hold me steady."

Inhale:
"You receive my heart."

Exhale:
"You hold me steady."

Take three natural breaths.

Lord, I pour out my heart before You. Thank You that I do not have to hide, perform, or explain everything perfectly. Be my refuge. Help me receive what I feel without being ruled by it. Lead me back into peace, wisdom, and trust.

You are my refuge.

Amen.
