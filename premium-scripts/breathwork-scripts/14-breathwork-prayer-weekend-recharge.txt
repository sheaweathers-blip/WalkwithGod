Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Weekend Recharge
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 8-10 minutes
Scripture: Exodus 33:14, "My presence will go with you, and I will give you rest."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Weekend Recharge.

Let your body settle into a restful position. You may sit, recline, or lie down. If you feel uncomfortable at any point, return to normal breathing.

This is not a performance.
This is receiving the rest of God's presence.

Our Scripture today is Exodus 33:14:
"My presence will go with you, and I will give you rest."

Take a slow breath in.
And let it go.

Again, breathe in.
And breathe out.

Let the breath become unforced.

Inhale:
"Your presence goes with me."

Exhale:
"You give me rest."

Inhale:
"Your presence goes with me."

Exhale:
"You give me rest."

Think back over the week.
Do not review it to judge yourself.
Review it with God.

What felt heavy?
What felt good?
Where did you notice His help?
Where did you need more grace?

Pause.

Now let your body release what does not need to be carried into the next week.

Inhale:
"You were with me."

Exhale:
"I release this week to You."

Inhale:
"You are with me."

Exhale:
"I receive Your rest."

Let there be a longer pause here.

Pause.

Take three natural breaths.

Lord, thank You that Your presence is my rest. Restore what has been stretched thin. Heal what has been strained. Teach me to rest without guilt and begin again without fear. Let this pause become worship.

Your presence goes with me.
You give me rest.

Amen.
