Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Rest
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 6-8 minutes
Scripture: Matthew 11:28, "Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Rest.

Choose a restful position. Let your body be supported. Let the breath be soft and unforced. If you begin to feel uncomfortable, breathe normally and rest.

Our Scripture today is Matthew 11:28:
"Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."

Take a gentle breath in.
And slowly breathe out.

Inhale:
"Jesus, I come to You."

Exhale:
"I receive Your rest."

Inhale:
"Jesus, I come to You."

Exhale:
"I receive Your rest."

Notice what feels heavy.
A responsibility.
A grief.
A pressure.
A tiredness.
A burden no one else sees.

Jesus does not shame the weary.
He invites them.

Inhale:
"You welcome the weary."

Exhale:
"I lay this down."

Inhale:
"You welcome the weary."

Exhale:
"I lay this down."

Let your jaw soften.
Let your shoulders release.
Let your hands rest.

You do not have to earn this rest.
You are invited to receive it.

Pause.

Take three natural breaths.

Jesus, I come to You weary and burdened. Teach me to receive rest instead of apologizing for needing it. Help me release what I cannot carry and trust You with what remains. Let my body and soul remember Your gentleness.

I come to You.
You give me rest.

Amen.
