Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Gratitude Prayer
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 5-7 minutes
Scripture: Psalm 107:1, "Oh give thanks to the Lord, for he is good, for his steadfast love endures forever."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Gratitude Prayer.

Settle into a comfortable posture. Let your breath become easy. If anything feels uncomfortable, return to natural breathing.

Gratitude is not denial of hardship.
Gratitude is remembering God's goodness in the middle of real life.

Our Scripture today is Psalm 107:1:
"Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Inhale:
"Thank You, Lord."

Exhale:
"Your love endures."

Inhale:
"Thank You, Lord."

Exhale:
"Your love endures."

Now name one gift from today.
Something simple.
Breath.
Food.
Light.
Mercy.
A person.
A moment of help.

Pause.

Inhale:
"Every good gift."

Exhale:
"Comes from You."

Inhale:
"Every good gift."

Exhale:
"Comes from You."

Now name one place where God carried you in the past.
Let memory become worship.

Pause.

Inhale:
"You have been faithful."

Exhale:
"I remember with thanks."

Take three quiet breaths.

Lord, thank You for Your steadfast love. Open my eyes to gifts I have rushed past. Let gratitude soften my heart, steady my mind, and turn my attention back to You. Even here, You are good.

Thank You, Lord.

Amen.
