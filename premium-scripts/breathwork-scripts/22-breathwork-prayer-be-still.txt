Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Be Still
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 5-7 minutes
Scripture: Psalm 46:10, "Be still, and know that I am God."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Be Still.

Settle into stillness as much as you are able. Let your hands rest. Let your face soften. If stillness feels difficult, that is okay. Simply stay gentle.

Our Scripture today is Psalm 46:10:
"Be still, and know that I am God."

Take a slow breath in.
And breathe out.

Again, breathe in.
And breathe out.

There is nothing to achieve in this moment.
Nothing to prove.
Nothing to rush.

Inhale:
"Be still."

Exhale:
"And know."

Inhale:
"Be still."

Exhale:
"And know."

Let the words become quieter.

Inhale:
"Be still."

Exhale:
"God is God."

Inhale:
"Be still."

Exhale:
"God is God."

Notice the urge to fill the space.
Notice the impulse to move on.
Notice the thoughts trying to take over.

Gently return:
Be still.
God is God.

Pause.

Now take three natural breaths without adding words.

Pause.

Lord, teach me stillness. Quiet the striving in me. Help me remember that I am not holding the world together. You are God. You are faithful. You are near. Let stillness become worship and trust.

Be still.
And know.

Amen.
