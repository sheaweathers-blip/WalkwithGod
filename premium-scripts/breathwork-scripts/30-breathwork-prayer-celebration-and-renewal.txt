Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Celebration and Renewal
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 8-10 minutes
Scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:17, "If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Celebration and Renewal.

Find a comfortable posture. Let your breath become steady. If anything feels uncomfortable, return to normal breathing.

This is a prayer of gratitude for what God has been forming across this breath prayer journey.

Our Scripture today is 2 Corinthians 5:17:
"If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Inhale:
"Christ makes me new."

Exhale:
"I receive His renewal."

Inhale:
"Christ makes me new."

Exhale:
"I receive His renewal."

Think back over this journey.
Moments of stillness.
Moments of peace.
Moments of honesty.
Moments of surrender.
Moments when you showed up even imperfectly.

Thank God for meeting you there.

Pause.

Inhale:
"Thank You for what has begun."

Exhale:
"Continue Your work in me."

Inhale:
"Thank You for what has begun."

Exhale:
"Continue Your work in me."

Now ask:
What do I want to carry forward?
What did God teach me about my body, breath, prayer, and pace?
Where is He inviting me to keep walking?

Pause.

Take three natural breaths.

Lord, thank You for every moment You met me in this journey. Thank You for stillness, breath, Scripture, prayer, and renewal. Let this not end as content completed, but continue as life with You. Make me new in Christ, one faithful step at a time.

You are still forming me.
I will keep walking with You.

Amen.
