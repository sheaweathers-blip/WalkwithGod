Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Focus Flow
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 5-7 minutes
Scripture: Colossians 3:23, "Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Focus Flow.

Find a comfortable seated or standing position. Let your eyes soften. Let your breath become easy. If the breathing feels uncomfortable, return to your natural rhythm.

This prayer is for returning your attention to God and the faithful work in front of you.

Our Scripture today is Colossians 3:23:
"Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Inhale:
"Lord, receive my attention."

Exhale:
"This work is for You."

Inhale:
"Lord, receive my attention."

Exhale:
"This work is for You."

Think of one task in front of you today.
Not the whole list.
Just one task.

Let that task become an offering instead of a pressure.

Inhale:
"One breath."

Exhale:
"One faithful task."

Inhale:
"One breath."

Exhale:
"One faithful task."

When distraction comes, do not fight it harshly.
Notice it.
Release it.
Return to God.

Again:
Notice.
Release.
Return.

Inhale:
"My mind returns to You."

Exhale:
"My hands serve You."

Inhale:
"My mind returns to You."

Exhale:
"My hands serve You."

Take three quiet breaths.

Pause.

Lord, help me work with a whole heart. Free me from performing for approval, rushing from fear, or scattering my attention in every direction. Let the next task become worship. Teach me to focus with peace, diligence, and love.

My work belongs to You.
My pace belongs to You.

Amen.
