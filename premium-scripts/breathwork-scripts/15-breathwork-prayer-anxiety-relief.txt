Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Anxiety Relief
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 5-7 minutes
Scripture: Philippians 4:6-7, "Do not be anxious about anything... and the peace of God... will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Anxiety Relief.

Find a supported position. Let your feet or back feel held. If slow breathing increases discomfort, return to normal breathing and simply listen.

This prayer does not ask you to pretend anxiety is not real.
It invites you to bring anxiety to God.

Our Scripture today is Philippians 4:6-7:
"Do not be anxious about anything... and the peace of God... will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus."

Breathe in for four.
Breathe out for six.

Inhale:
"God, I bring this to You."

Exhale:
"Guard my heart and mind."

Inhale:
"God, I bring this to You."

Exhale:
"Guard my heart and mind."

Now name one anxious concern.
Just one.

Pause.

You do not need to solve it while you pray.
You are allowed to be honest.
You are allowed to need help.

Inhale:
"You are near."

Exhale:
"This belongs in Your hands."

Inhale:
"You are near."

Exhale:
"This belongs in Your hands."

Imagine God's peace standing guard.
Not fragile peace.
Not fake peace.
The peace of Christ guarding what feels vulnerable in you.

Take three slow breaths.

Pause.

God of peace, I bring You what I cannot control. Guard my heart. Guard my mind. Teach me to pray instead of spiral, breathe instead of rush, and trust You for wisdom one step at a time.

Your peace is stronger than this fear.

Amen.
