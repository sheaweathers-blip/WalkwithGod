Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Patience
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 4-6 minutes
Scripture: Galatians 5:22, "The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience..."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Patience.

Settle into a comfortable posture. Let the breath be slow and easy. If waiting between breaths feels stressful, breathe naturally.

Patience is not passive frustration.
Patience is fruit grown by the Holy Spirit.

Our Scripture today is Galatians 5:22:
"The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience..."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Inhale:
"Holy Spirit, grow patience."

Exhale:
"Slow my hurry."

Inhale:
"Holy Spirit, grow patience."

Exhale:
"Slow my hurry."

Notice where impatience has been showing up.
In your schedule.
In a relationship.
In traffic.
In waiting for an answer.
In waiting for yourself to change.

Bring that place before God.

Inhale:
"I can wait with You."

Exhale:
"I do not have to force."

Inhale:
"I can wait with You."

Exhale:
"I do not have to force."

Patience does not mean doing nothing.
It means refusing to let hurry rule your spirit.

Ask God:
What is mine to do?
What is mine to release?

Pause.

Take three natural breaths.

Holy Spirit, grow patience in me. Help me wait without resentment, act without rushing, and trust without demanding control. Let patience become love in motion today.

I can wait with You.

Amen.
