Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Forgiveness
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 6-8 minutes
Scripture: Ephesians 4:32, "Be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, as God in Christ forgave you."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Forgiveness.

Find a supported position. Let your breath stay gentle. If this topic feels tender, move slowly. Forgiveness can be a process, and wisdom and boundaries still matter.

Our Scripture today is Ephesians 4:32:
"Be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, as God in Christ forgave you."

Take a slow breath in.
And slowly breathe out.

Inhale:
"I have received mercy."

Exhale:
"Lead me toward freedom."

Inhale:
"I have received mercy."

Exhale:
"Lead me toward freedom."

Bring to mind a wound, a resentment, or a debt you have been rehearsing.
You do not have to force emotion.
You do not have to say what is not true.
You are simply bringing it before Jesus.

Pause.

Inhale:
"Jesus, meet me here."

Exhale:
"I release revenge to You."

Inhale:
"Jesus, meet me here."

Exhale:
"I release revenge to You."

Forgiveness does not mean pretending harm did not happen.
It does not mean avoiding wise help.
It does not mean removing all boundaries.

Forgiveness begins by placing justice, healing, and the person who hurt you in God's hands.

Inhale:
"Your mercy holds me."

Exhale:
"Your wisdom leads me."

Take three quiet breaths.

Lord Jesus, You have forgiven me with costly love. Lead me toward forgiveness that is honest, wise, and free. Heal what has been wounded. Release me from bitterness. Teach me how to walk in mercy and truth.

Amen.
