Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Strength
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 5-7 minutes
Scripture: Isaiah 40:31, "They who wait for the Lord shall renew their strength."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Strength.

Find a steady posture. Let your body be supported. If you are tired, you may practice lying down. Return to normal breathing if needed.

This prayer is not about forcing strength.
It is about receiving strength from the Lord.

Our Scripture today is Isaiah 40:31:
"They who wait for the Lord shall renew their strength."

Take a slow breath in.
And breathe out.

Inhale:
"I wait on You."

Exhale:
"Renew my strength."

Inhale:
"I wait on You."

Exhale:
"Renew my strength."

Notice where you feel weary.
In your body.
In your emotions.
In your faith.
In your responsibilities.

You do not have to pretend you are not tired.
Bring the weariness to God.

Inhale:
"You see my weakness."

Exhale:
"You give what I need."

Inhale:
"You see my weakness."

Exhale:
"You give what I need."

Ask God for strength for the next step.
Not the whole month.
Not the whole future.
Just the next faithful step.

Pause.

Take three steady breaths.

Lord, renew my strength as I wait on You. Help me stop forcing what only You can give. Strengthen my body, my mind, my faith, and my obedience. Give me enough grace for the next step.

I wait on You.

Amen.
