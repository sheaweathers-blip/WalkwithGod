Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Walking With God
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 8-10 minutes
Scripture: Micah 6:8, "What does the Lord require of you but to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God?"

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Walking With God.

You may do this seated, standing, or while walking slowly in a safe place. If you walk, keep your eyes open and stay aware of your surroundings.

Our Scripture today is Micah 6:8:
"Do justice, love kindness, and walk humbly with your God."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

If you are walking, let your pace be easy.

Inhale:
"Walk humbly."

Exhale:
"With my God."

Inhale:
"Walk humbly."

Exhale:
"With my God."

Notice your steps.
Notice your breath.
Notice that God is not only present in quiet rooms.
He is present in the movement of ordinary life.

Inhale:
"Do justice."

Exhale:
"Love kindness."

Inhale:
"Walk humbly."

Exhale:
"With my God."

Ask God:
Where can I practice justice today?
Where can I show kindness today?
Where do I need humility today?

Pause.

If you are walking, take several steps in silence.
If you are seated, take several breaths in silence.

Pause.

Lord, teach me to walk with You in ordinary places. Let my faith become justice, kindness, humility, and love. Help my body, breath, choices, and conversations move with You today.

I do not walk alone.
I walk with You.

Amen.
