Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Clear Thinking
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 4-6 minutes
Scripture: James 1:5, "If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Clear Thinking.

Find a comfortable position. Let your hands rest easily. Unclench your jaw. If any breath pattern feels uncomfortable, return to normal breathing.

This practice is a way to slow down and ask God for wisdom.

Our Scripture today is James 1:5:
"If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God."

Take a natural breath in.
And slowly breathe out.

We will use a simple box breath.
Breathe in for four.
Hold gently for four.
Breathe out for four.
Hold gently for four.

Only hold if it feels comfortable.

Inhale:
"God of wisdom."

Hold:
"I am listening."

Exhale:
"Order my thoughts."

Hold:
"Lead me in peace."

Again.

Inhale:
"God of wisdom."

Hold:
"I am listening."

Exhale:
"Order my thoughts."

Hold:
"Lead me in peace."

Now bring one decision, one task, or one mental knot before God.
Do not try to solve it all right now.
Simply name it in His presence.

Pause.

Ask Him for wisdom without shame.
God is generous.
God is not annoyed by your need.
God is not confused by your confusion.

Inhale:
"Give me wisdom."

Exhale:
"Make the next step clear."

Inhale:
"Give me wisdom."

Exhale:
"Make the next step clear."

Take three quiet breaths.

Pause.

Lord, I ask for wisdom. Clear away panic, pride, distraction, and fear. Help me think with humility and courage. Guide my choices through Your Word, Your Spirit, wise counsel, and the fruit of peace. I do not need every answer at once. I trust You for the next faithful step.

Amen.
