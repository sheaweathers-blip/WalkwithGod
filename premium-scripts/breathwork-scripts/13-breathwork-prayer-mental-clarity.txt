Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Mental Clarity
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 5-7 minutes
Scripture: Romans 12:2, "Be transformed by the renewal of your mind."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Mental Clarity.

Find a quiet posture. Let your breath move gently through your nose if that is comfortable. If not, breathe in the way that feels natural.

This prayer is about allowing God to renew your mind with truth.

Our Scripture today is Romans 12:2:
"Be transformed by the renewal of your mind."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Inhale:
"Renew my mind."

Exhale:
"Anchor me in truth."

Inhale:
"Renew my mind."

Exhale:
"Anchor me in truth."

Notice the thoughts moving through you.
Some may be useful.
Some may be anxious.
Some may be harsh.
Some may be old stories.

You do not have to chase every thought.
You can bring them before God.

Inhale:
"This thought is seen."

Exhale:
"God, show me what is true."

Inhale:
"This thought is seen."

Exhale:
"God, show me what is true."

Now name one thought that has been shaping your mood or choices.
Hold it before the Lord.

Ask:
Is this true?
Is this loving?
Is this from fear?
Does this agree with Scripture?

Pause.

Inhale:
"Your truth renews me."

Exhale:
"Your peace steadies me."

Take three natural breaths.

Lord, renew my mind. Help me recognize lies, fear, shame, and confusion. Replace them with truth that leads to love, obedience, wisdom, and peace. Let my thoughts become a place where Your Word is welcome.

Anchor me in what is true.

Amen.
