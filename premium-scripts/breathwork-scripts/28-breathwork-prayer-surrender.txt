Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Surrender
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 6-8 minutes
Scripture: Luke 22:42, "Nevertheless, not my will, but yours, be done."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Surrender.

Find a comfortable posture. If you can, rest your hands open. If anything feels uncomfortable, return to natural breathing.

Surrender is not giving up on God.
Surrender is trusting God with what you cannot control.

Our Scripture today is Luke 22:42:
"Not my will, but yours, be done."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Inhale:
"Father, I trust You."

Exhale:
"Into Your hands."

Inhale:
"Father, I trust You."

Exhale:
"Into Your hands."

Now name one thing you have been trying to hold tightly.
An outcome.
A person.
A plan.
A fear.
A timeline.

Bring it before God.

Inhale:
"This matters to me."

Exhale:
"I place it before You."

Inhale:
"This matters to me."

Exhale:
"I place it before You."

Surrender does not mean you stop caring.
It means you stop pretending you are God.

Inhale:
"Not my will."

Exhale:
"But Yours be done."

Inhale:
"Not my will."

Exhale:
"But Yours be done."

Pause.

Lord, teach me surrender. Help me obey what is mine to do and release what belongs in Your hands. Make my heart willing, honest, and humble. I trust Your wisdom more than my control.

Not my will.
Yours be done.

Amen.
