Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Hope
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 5-7 minutes
Scripture: Romans 15:13, "May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Hope.

Sit or stand comfortably. Let your breathing become steady. If you feel uncomfortable, return to normal breathing.

Hope in Scripture is not pretending everything is easy.
Hope is confidence in the God who is faithful.

Our Scripture today is Romans 15:13:
"May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Inhale:
"God of hope."

Exhale:
"Fill me again."

Inhale:
"God of hope."

Exhale:
"Fill me again."

Notice where discouragement has been sitting in you.
Maybe a delay.
A loss.
A disappointment.
A fear about the future.

Bring that place honestly to God.

Inhale:
"I trust You here."

Exhale:
"Renew my hope."

Inhale:
"I trust You here."

Exhale:
"Renew my hope."

Hope does not require you to know how everything works out.
Hope begins by remembering who God is.

He is faithful.
He is merciful.
He is present.
He is not finished.

Take three natural breaths.

Pause.

God of hope, fill me with joy and peace as I trust You. Let hope rise where discouragement has been heavy. Strengthen me for the next step. Help me believe that You are still working, still present, and still good.

My hope is in You.

Amen.
