Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Trust
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 5-7 minutes
Scripture: Proverbs 3:5-6, "Trust in the Lord with all your heart... and he will make straight your paths."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Trust.

Settle into a comfortable position. Let your breathing be gentle. If any part of this feels uncomfortable, return to normal breathing.

Trust does not require knowing every detail.
Trust means placing your heart in God's hands.

Our Scripture today is Proverbs 3:5-6:
"Trust in the Lord with all your heart... and he will make straight your paths."

Take a breath in.
And slowly breathe out.

Inhale:
"I trust You."

Exhale:
"You lead my path."

Inhale:
"I trust You."

Exhale:
"You lead my path."

Now notice where you have been leaning on your own understanding.
Trying to predict.
Trying to control.
Trying to solve every outcome.

Bring that to God.

Inhale:
"I do not have to know everything."

Exhale:
"I can follow You today."

Inhale:
"I do not have to know everything."

Exhale:
"I can follow You today."

Ask God:
What is the next faithful step?
Not the whole road.
Just the next step.

Pause.

Take three natural breaths.

Lord, I trust You with all my heart. Forgive me for leaning only on what I can understand. Make my path straight as I acknowledge You. Give me courage to obey the next step without demanding the whole map.

You lead.
I will follow.

Amen.
