Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Productivity Reset
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 4-6 minutes
Scripture: Psalm 90:12, "So teach us to number our days that we may get a heart of wisdom."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Productivity Reset.

Sit comfortably. Let your shoulders drop. Let your hands unclench. If holding the breath feels uncomfortable, skip the hold and breathe naturally.

This is a reset for hurry.
This is a prayer for wise time, not frantic time.

Our Scripture today is Psalm 90:12:
"So teach us to number our days that we may get a heart of wisdom."

Breathe in for four.
Hold gently for four.
Exhale for six.

Inhale:
"Teach me wisdom."

Hold:
"My time is Yours."

Exhale:
"Release my hurry."

Again.

Inhale:
"Teach me wisdom."

Hold:
"My time is Yours."

Exhale:
"Release my hurry."

Now think about your day.
Notice the pressure.
Notice the expectations.
Notice the tasks that feel loud.

You are not a machine.
You are a person made by God.
Your limits are not a failure.
Your need for wisdom is not weakness.

Inhale:
"Order my day."

Exhale:
"Lead my next step."

Inhale:
"Order my day."

Exhale:
"Lead my next step."

Ask God:
What matters most today?
What can wait?
What needs obedience instead of anxiety?

Pause.

Take three natural breaths.

Lord, teach me to number my days. Help me stop treating time like something I own apart from You. Give me wisdom to work, rest, serve, and say no with peace. Order my steps so productivity becomes faithfulness, not frantic striving.

My time is Yours.

Amen.
