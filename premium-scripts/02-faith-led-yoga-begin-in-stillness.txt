Walk With God Premium Script
Title: Begin in Stillness
Category: Faith-Led Yoga
Estimated Length: 12-15 minutes
Scripture: Psalm 46:10, "Be still, and know that I am God."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's faith-led movement session is called Begin in Stillness.

Move gently and listen to your body. Skip anything that does not feel safe or comfortable. This practice is not about performance. It is about offering your body, breath, and attention to God.

Begin seated or kneeling.

Let your hands rest on your thighs.
Close your eyes if comfortable.

Our Scripture is Psalm 46:10:
"Be still, and know that I am God."

Take a slow breath in.
And breathe out.

Before we move, pray quietly:
"Lord, I am here with You."

Begin with gentle neck rolls.
Move slowly.
Let go of tension in the jaw, shoulders, and face.

Now inhale and lift your shoulders toward your ears.
Exhale and release them down.
Again, inhale up.
Exhale, release.

Come onto hands and knees for tabletop position.

As you inhale, lift your chest gently.
As you exhale, round your back.
This is slow cat-cow movement.

Inhale, open.
Exhale, round.

As you move, pray:
"God, awaken my body to Your presence."

Now press back into child's pose, or a comfortable resting shape.
Let your forehead lower if possible.
Let your arms rest forward or by your sides.

Breathe here.

This is not a collapse.
This is surrender.

Quietly pray:
"Lord, I surrender what I cannot carry."

Come slowly back to tabletop.

Step one foot forward into a low lunge.
Keep your hands on the floor, blocks, or your thigh.
Let your hips soften.

Pray:
"Guide my steps today."

Switch sides slowly.

Step the other foot forward.
Breathe.
No rushing.

Pray:
"Help me walk in Your way."

Now come to standing.

Stand tall in mountain pose.
Feet grounded.
Arms relaxed.
Heart lifted.

Take a breath in and reach your arms overhead.
Exhale, lower your hands to your heart.

Again, inhale, reach.
Exhale, hands to heart.

Pray:
"My strength comes from You."

Take a gentle forward fold.
Bend your knees as much as needed.
Let your head and neck relax.

Ask God:
"What do I need to release before I continue today?"

Slowly roll up to standing.

Bring your hands to your heart.

We'll close with one final breath prayer.

Inhale:
"Be still."

Exhale:
"You are God."

Again:
"Be still."
"You are God."

Lord, thank You for the body You created.
Help me care for it without shame, comparison, or pressure.
Let my movement become worship.
Let my stillness become trust.
Let my day become a walk with You.

Amen.
