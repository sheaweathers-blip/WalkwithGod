Walk With God Abide
Faith-Led Yoga Session 3: Strength Renewed
Length: About 25 minutes
Style: Strength and balance
Scripture: Isaiah 40:31

Safety note:
This session includes gentle strengthening and balance. Keep a wall or chair nearby if helpful. Strength is not strain. Rest whenever you need.

Opening:
Welcome to Strength Renewed.

Isaiah 40:31 says, "But they who wait for the Lord shall renew their strength."

Today we will practice steady strength while remembering that renewal comes from waiting on God, not forcing our way forward.

Stand or sit tall. Take a breath in.
Slowly breathe out.

Breath prayer:
Inhale: I wait on You.
Exhale: Renew my strength.

Mountain pose:
Come to standing. Ground through your feet. Let your hands rest at your sides.

Notice where you feel tired. Notice where you feel capable. Bring both honestly before God.

Chair pose:
Bend your knees slightly. Sit the hips back. Keep the chest lifted. Hold for three slow breaths.

Pray:
Lord, strengthen what is weak in me.

Rise slowly. Rest.

Repeat chair one more time, choosing a smaller shape if needed.

Warrior one:
Step one foot back. Turn the back foot slightly out. Bend the front knee. Reach arms up or keep hands at the heart.

Stay steady.

Say:
My strength is renewed in You.

Switch sides.

Warrior two:
Open to the side. Front knee bent, arms reaching forward and back, gaze soft.

This is not about looking strong. This is about practicing faithfulness in your body.

Ask:
Where am I trying to force what God is asking me to entrust?

Switch sides.

Tree pose:
Stand near a wall if needed. Bring one foot to the ankle, calf, or inner thigh, avoiding the knee. Hands can stay at the heart.

Balance is allowed to wobble.

Pray:
Keep me rooted in You.

Switch sides.

Bridge pose:
Lie on your back with knees bent and feet on the floor. Press gently through the feet and lift the hips. Keep the neck relaxed.

Hold for three breaths.

Lower slowly.

Repeat if it feels good.

Reclined hamstring stretch:
Extend one leg upward or hold behind the thigh. Keep the stretch gentle. Breathe.

Switch sides.

Final rest:
Lie down comfortably. Let your body receive rest after effort.

Hear the promise again:
They who wait for the Lord shall renew their strength.

Closing prayer:
Renew my strength, Lord, and teach me to depend on You rather than force. Help me wait with trust and move with wisdom. Give me strength for the next faithful step. Amen.
