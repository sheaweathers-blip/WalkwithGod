Walk With God Abide
Faith-Led Yoga Session 4: Peace for Anxiety
Length: About 22 minutes
Style: Calming restorative
Scripture: Philippians 4:6-7

Safety note:
This is a slow restorative practice. If anxiety increases while focusing on breath, let go of the breath pattern and simply notice the support beneath you.

Opening:
Welcome to Peace for Anxiety.

Philippians 4:6-7 invites us to bring everything to God by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving, and promises that the peace of God will guard our hearts and minds in Christ Jesus.

You do not have to pretend anxiety is not real. You are invited to bring it to God.

Hand-on-heart breathing:
Place one hand on your heart and one hand on your belly, or rest your hands wherever comfortable.

Inhale gently.
Exhale slowly.

Pray:
God is near.
I bring this to You.

Seated fold:
Sit tall, then fold forward over crossed legs, extended legs, or a cushion. Let your head be supported if possible.

Name one concern quietly before God.

Say:
Lord, this is what feels heavy.

Supported child's pose:
Come into child's pose with a pillow or folded blanket under your chest, or rest forward onto a chair.

Let your body feel supported.

Pray:
I am not carrying this alone.

Stay for several slow breaths.

Low supported lunge:
Step one foot forward. Keep the back knee down. Use blocks, a chair, or your thigh for support. Let the shape be easy.

Ask:
What is mine to do today, and what belongs in God's hands?

Switch sides slowly.

Legs up the wall or calves on chair:
Come onto your back. Place legs up a wall or calves on a chair. If neither works, rest with knees bent.

Let your arms relax.

Imagine the peace of God guarding your heart and mind, not like a wall of fear, but like the faithful presence of Christ.

Pray:
Guard my heart.
Guard my mind.
Keep me in Christ Jesus.

Long rest:
Stay here. No fixing. No performing. Just receiving.

If your mind wanders, return to:
God is near.
I bring this to You.

Closing prayer:
God of peace, guard my heart and mind in Christ Jesus. Receive what I cannot control. Give me wisdom for what is mine to do, courage to ask for help when needed, and trust for what belongs in Your hands. Amen.
