Walk With God Abide
Faith-Led Yoga Session 1: Begin in Stillness
Length: About 20 minutes
Style: Gentle flow
Scripture: Psalm 46:10

Safety note:
Move slowly and gently. Nothing in this session should cause pain. Modify, pause, or rest at any time. This is not about performance. This is a prayerful way to care for the body God created.

Opening:
Welcome to Begin in Stillness.

Find a comfortable seated position on your mat, a folded blanket, or a chair. Let your hands rest open. Let your shoulders soften. Let your jaw unclench.

Psalm 46:10 says, "Be still, and know that I am God."

Today we are not trying to earn peace. We are making room to notice the God who is already near.

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Pray quietly:
Lord, quiet my striving.
Help my body and soul become still before You.

Seated breath prayer:
Inhale: Be still.
Exhale: You are God.

Repeat that for several breaths.

Neck and shoulder release:
Let your right ear drift toward your right shoulder. Keep the stretch gentle. Breathe.

Come back through center.
Let your left ear drift toward your left shoulder. Breathe.

Roll your shoulders slowly up, back, and down. Again, up, back, and down.

As you release tension, pray:
God, I release hurry.
God, I receive Your presence.

Tabletop and cat-cow:
Come to hands and knees, or stay seated and round and lift through the spine.

Inhale, lift the chest gently.
Exhale, round the back.

Move slowly for a few rounds.

Let each movement become prayer:
With my breath, I remember You.
With my body, I return to You.

Child's pose:
Bring your hips back toward your heels, or rest forward on a chair or cushion. Let your forehead soften. If this shape does not work for your body, choose any restful position.

Whisper:
I do not have to rush to be held by God.

Low lunge:
Step one foot forward. Keep the back knee down. Let your hands rest on the mat, blocks, or your front thigh. Breathe gently.

Ask:
Lord, what pace are You inviting me to walk in today?

Switch sides slowly. Let the second side be just as gentle.

Forward fold:
Come to standing if that feels good. Fold forward with soft knees. Let your head and arms be heavy, or rest your hands on your legs or a chair.

As you fold, pray:
I bow my plans before You.
Lead me in Your way.

Gentle twist:
Come seated or lying down. Twist gently to one side. Keep the breath smooth.

Pray:
Turn my attention back to You.

Switch sides.

Supported rest:
Come to a comfortable resting position. Let your palms face up if that feels right.

Hear the Scripture again:
Be still, and know that I am God.

Silence for reflection.

Closing prayer:
Lord, quiet my striving and help my body and soul become still before You. Teach me to begin with Your presence, not pressure. Let this stillness follow me into the rest of my day. Amen.
