Walk With God Abide
Faith-Led Yoga Session 2: Morning Mercy Flow
Length: About 18 minutes
Style: Slow energizing flow
Scripture: Lamentations 3:22-23

Safety note:
Move with kindness toward your body. Keep the pace slow. Skip any movement that does not feel safe today.

Opening:
Welcome to Morning Mercy Flow.

Lamentations 3:22-23 says, "The steadfast love of the Lord never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning."

Before the demands of the day have the first word, let mercy have the first word.

Sit tall. Rest your hands open.

Inhale: Your mercies are new.
Exhale: I begin again with You.

Seated breath and sun-breath arms:
Inhale, reach your arms slowly out and up.
Exhale, lower your hands back down.

Move like you are receiving a gift, not forcing energy.

Pray:
Thank You for new mercy today.

Tabletop mobility:
Come to hands and knees, or stay seated and move through your spine. Circle your shoulders. Circle your hips gently. Let the body wake up slowly.

Say quietly:
God, thank You for breath.
Thank You for this morning.
Thank You for one more beginning.

Low lunge:
Step one foot forward. Let the back knee rest down. Inhale and lift the chest. Exhale and soften the shoulders.

Ask:
Where do I need to receive mercy instead of pressure today?

Half split:
Shift your hips back gently and lengthen the front leg as much as feels comfortable. Keep a soft bend in the knee. Breathe.

Pray:
Help me move wisely, not hurriedly.

Switch sides. Low lunge first, then half split.

Mountain pose:
Come to standing. Feet grounded. Hands at your sides.

Feel the steadiness of the floor beneath you.

Inhale: God's mercy is new.
Exhale: I can begin again.

Standing side stretch:
Reach one arm overhead and lean gently to the side. Breathe into the ribs. Come back through center and switch sides.

Slow chair pose:
Bend your knees slightly, as if sitting back into a chair. Keep it small. Let your arms reach forward or hands come to prayer.

Stay for two breaths.

Pray:
Give me strength for today's faithful steps.

Restful forward fold:
Fold forward with soft knees. Let the head release. Let the day rest in God's hands.

Say:
This day belongs to You.

Seated rest:
Come down to a seated or lying position. Place one hand over your heart.

Remember:
Mercy is not something you have to manufacture. It is something God gives.

Closing prayer:
Thank You for new mercy today. Help me walk in it with humility and joy. Let my words, choices, work, and relationships be shaped by Your faithfulness. Amen.
