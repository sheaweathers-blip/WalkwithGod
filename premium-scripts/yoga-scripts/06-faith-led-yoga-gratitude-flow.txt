Walk With God Abide
Faith-Led Yoga Session 6: Gratitude Flow
Length: About 20 minutes
Style: Gentle standing flow
Scripture: Psalm 107:1

Safety note:
Move at a comfortable pace. Keep the flow gentle and accessible. Gratitude is not denial; it is attention to God's goodness.

Opening:
Welcome to Gratitude Flow.

Psalm 107:1 says, "Oh give thanks to the Lord, for he is good, for his steadfast love endures forever."

Today we let movement become thanksgiving for the body, breath, and day God has given.

Seated breath prayer:
Inhale: Lord, You are good.
Exhale: Thank You for this gift.

Name one gift quietly.

Standing side stretch:
Come to standing. Reach one arm up and lean gently to the side.

Thank God for breath.

Switch sides.

Slow sun-breaths:
Inhale, reach arms out and up.
Exhale, bring hands down through center.

Repeat several times.

With each round, name a gift:
Mercy.
Shelter.
Friendship.
Provision.
Forgiveness.
Beauty.

Warrior two:
Step wide. Bend one knee and reach arms open.

Let the shape be steady, not strained.

Pray:
Let gratitude make me generous.

Peaceful warrior:
Rest back hand gently on the back leg and reach the front arm up. Keep the breath easy.

Thank God for one ordinary thing you often overlook.

Switch sides: warrior two, then peaceful warrior.

Wide fold:
Turn toes forward and fold with soft knees. Hands can rest on the floor, blocks, a chair, or your legs.

Say:
All I have is received.

Seated twist:
Come seated. Twist gently to one side.

Ask:
How can gratitude reshape my attitude today?

Switch sides.

Final rest:
Lie down or sit comfortably. Let gratitude settle into your body.

Pray slowly:
Thank You, Lord.
Thank You, Lord.
Thank You, Lord.

Closing prayer:
Thank You, Lord, for Your steadfast love and for this body You created. Teach me to notice Your gifts and live today with humility, joy, and generosity. Amen.
