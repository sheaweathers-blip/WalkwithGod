Faith-Led Yoga Session 7: Rest for the Weary
Length: About 30 minutes
Style: Restorative
Scripture: Matthew 11:28

Safety note:
Use pillows, blankets, a chair, or the wall for support. This session is meant to be restful. You can remain in one comfortable position the entire time if that is what your body needs.

Opening:
Welcome to Rest for the Weary.

Jesus says in Matthew 11:28, "Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."

You do not have to earn rest. You do not have to apologize for needing it. Jesus invites the weary to come.

Supported child's pose:
Rest your body forward over a pillow, cushion, or chair. Let your arms be comfortable.

Inhale: Jesus, I come to You.
Exhale: I receive Your rest.

Stay here for several breaths.

Reclined butterfly:
Lie on your back. Bring the soles of the feet together and let knees fall open, supported by pillows if needed. Or rest with knees bent.

Place one hand on your heart.

Say:
My weariness is welcome with Jesus.

Gentle twist:
Bring knees to one side. Support them if needed. Let your shoulders soften.

Pray:
Release what I do not need to carry into rest.

Switch sides slowly.

Supported bridge:
Place a pillow or folded blanket under your hips, or simply rest flat on your back. Let the front body soften.

Ask:
Where have I confused rest with laziness?
Where is Jesus inviting me to receive?

Legs on chair:
Place calves on a chair or couch, or keep knees bent. Let the floor hold you.

Take a long pause.

Breath prayer:
Inhale: Jesus, I come.
Exhale: You give rest.

Extended stillness:
Remain in the most comfortable resting shape. Let the body become quiet.

If thoughts come, do not fight them. Offer them to Jesus and return to the prayer:
Jesus, I come.
You give rest.

Closing prayer:
Jesus, I come to You weary and receive the rest You freely offer. Teach me to live from Your gentleness instead of my striving. Restore my body, mind, and soul in Your care. Amen.
