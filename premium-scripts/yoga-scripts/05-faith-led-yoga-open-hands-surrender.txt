Walk With God Abide
Faith-Led Yoga Session 5: Open Hands Surrender
Length: About 20 minutes
Style: Gentle mobility
Scripture: 1 Peter 5:7

Safety note:
Move gently. Let surrender be prayer, not pressure. If a posture does not feel right, choose rest.

Opening:
Welcome to Open Hands Surrender.

1 Peter 5:7 says, "Casting all your anxieties on him, because he cares for you."

Today we practice opening the hands, softening the body, and releasing what we were not meant to carry alone.

Seated open hands breath:
Sit comfortably. Rest your hands on your thighs with palms up.

Inhale: Father, You care for me.
Exhale: I release this to You.

Repeat for several breaths.

Shoulder rolls:
Roll the shoulders up, back, and down. Move slowly.

As the shoulders release, imagine setting down one burden.

Say:
This is not mine to carry alone.

Cat-cow:
Come to hands and knees or seated spinal movement.

Inhale, open the chest.
Exhale, round the back.

Let each exhale become surrender.

Thread the needle:
From tabletop, slide one arm underneath the other and rest shoulder or head toward the mat or a support. Keep it gentle.

Pray:
Lord, untangle what has become tight in me.

Switch sides.

Low lunge with open palms:
Step one foot forward. Let the back knee rest down. Bring palms open on the front thigh or at your sides.

Ask:
What care keeps returning to my mind?

Pray:
I give this to You again.

Switch sides.

Forward fold:
Stand or sit and fold forward. Bend the knees generously.

Let your hands hang or open toward the floor.

Say:
I release control.
I receive Your care.

Supported rest:
Come to a restful position. Maybe place a pillow under your knees or chest.

Let your hands stay open.

Silence.

Closing prayer:
Father, I cast my cares on You because You care for me. Teach me the difference between faithful responsibility and anxious control. Help me leave in Your hands what belongs to You. Amen.
