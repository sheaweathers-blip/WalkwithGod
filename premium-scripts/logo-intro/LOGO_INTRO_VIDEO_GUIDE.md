# Walk With God Logo Intro Video

Use this as the opening for premium breathwork, walking, yoga, and Bible study videos.

## Files To Use

- Title card: `premium-scripts/logo-intro/logo-intro-title-card.png`
- Ending card: `premium-scripts/logo-intro/logo-intro-end-card.png`
- Logo: `assets/walk-with-god-logo.png`
- Main breathwork audio: `premium-media/breathwork-day-1.wav`
- Suggested music bed: `assets/music/living-waters.mp3` or `assets/music/nature-breath.mp3`

## Recommended Length

8 to 10 seconds.

## Simple Canva Build

1. Open Canva and choose **Video 16:9**.
2. Upload `logo-intro-title-card.png`.
3. Put it on the timeline for **7 seconds**.
4. Add a fade-in at the beginning, around **1 second**.
5. Add a fade-out at the end, around **1 second**.
6. Add `Living Waters` or `Nature Breath` as the music.
7. Set music volume to about **12-18%** for the intro.
8. Export as **MP4, 1080p**.

## Optional Full Intro Timeline

- 0.0-1.0 seconds: fade in from soft green/cream
- 1.0-4.5 seconds: logo/title held still
- 4.5-7.0 seconds: very gentle zoom in
- 7.0-8.5 seconds: fade out

## Text Style

Keep it minimal:

- Walk With God
- Premium Breathwork Prayer
- Simply Breathe

Avoid adding a lot of extra instructions on the intro. Let the session itself teach.

## Best Music Choices

- `Living Waters`: peaceful, reflective, devotional
- `Nature Breath`: calm wellness feel
- `Sunlit Dream`: warmer and brighter

## For Vertical Phone Videos

Create a separate **Mobile Video 9:16** version in Canva.

Use the same title card as the visual reference, but rebuild the text/logo centered for vertical so nothing gets cropped.

## Finished File Name

Use:

`walk-with-god-logo-intro.mp4`

For the first breathwork video:

`simply-breathe-premium-breathwork.mp4`
