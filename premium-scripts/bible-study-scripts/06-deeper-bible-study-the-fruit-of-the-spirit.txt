Walk With God Abide
Deeper Bible Study 6: The Fruit of the Spirit
Length: About 35 minutes
Format: Character study
Primary Scripture: Galatians 5:22-25

Opening:
Welcome to The Fruit of the Spirit.

Spiritual growth is not self-improvement with religious language. It is the Spirit forming the character of Christ in us.

Pray:
Holy Spirit, form the character of Christ in me. Amen.

Read Galatians 5:22-25.

Teaching:
Paul lists the fruit of the Spirit: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control.

Notice that Paul says fruit, not fruits. These qualities belong together as the Spirit's work in a surrendered life.

Fruit grows from life with God.
A tree does not produce fruit by straining harder in isolation. Fruit grows from roots, nourishment, time, pruning, and life. In the same way, Spirit-formed character grows as we abide in Christ and walk by the Spirit.

Fruit is relational and visible.
Love is tested with people. Patience is tested in delay. Kindness is tested when we are inconvenienced. Self-control is tested when desire pulls hard. The fruit of the Spirit becomes visible in ordinary pressure.

Fruit is not personality.
Gentleness is not only for quiet people. Joy is not only for cheerful people. Faithfulness is not only for naturally disciplined people. This is the Spirit's character-forming work in every believer.

Fruit often grows through surrender.
Paul says those who belong to Christ have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires. Growth involves saying no to the old way and yes to the Spirit.

Guided reflection:
Read the list slowly.
Love.
Joy.
Peace.
Patience.
Kindness.
Goodness.
Faithfulness.
Gentleness.
Self-control.

Which one feels most needed in this season?
Which one is being tested?
Which one do you try to produce in your own strength?

Practice:
Choose one fruit and one relationship. Ask: What would this fruit look like today in words, tone, timing, boundaries, or action?

Reflection questions:
Where is pressure revealing what still needs formation?
How can I keep in step with the Spirit today?
What is one old pattern I need to surrender?

Closing prayer:
Holy Spirit, form the character of Christ in me. Grow fruit that is visible in ordinary life, especially under pressure. Teach me to keep in step with You. Amen.
