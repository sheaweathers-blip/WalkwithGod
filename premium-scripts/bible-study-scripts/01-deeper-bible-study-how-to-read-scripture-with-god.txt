Deeper Bible Study 1: How to Read Scripture With God
Length: About 35 minutes
Format: Teaching and reflection
Primary Scripture: 2 Timothy 3:16-17

Opening:
Welcome to this deeper Bible study: How to Read Scripture With God.

Before we talk about method, we begin with posture. We are not opening Scripture to master God. We are opening Scripture to be formed by God.

Pray:
Lord, open my eyes to Your word. Teach me to read with reverence, humility, attention, and obedience. Amen.

Read 2 Timothy 3:16-17.

Teaching:
Paul writes that all Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, reproof, correction, and training in righteousness. That means Scripture is not merely inspirational. It is formative. It teaches what is true, exposes what is false, corrects what is crooked, and trains us into a life that reflects God.

A helpful pattern for reading Scripture with God is: context, attention, revelation, response.

First: context.
Ask, "What is happening here?" Who is speaking? Who is listening? Is this narrative, poetry, prophecy, wisdom, gospel, letter, or apocalyptic writing? Context protects us from turning Scripture into disconnected quotes.

Second: attention.
Read slowly. Notice repeated words, commands, promises, warnings, questions, images, and contrasts. Do not rush past what feels familiar.

Third: revelation.
Ask, "What does this reveal about God?" Scripture is not first a mirror for self-improvement. It is first a witness to who God is, what He has done, what He loves, what He commands, and how He relates to His people.

Fourth: response.
Ask, "What faithful response is invited?" The response may be worship, confession, obedience, patience, repentance, courage, forgiveness, service, or rest.

Guided practice:
Read Psalm 1 slowly.

Ask:
What images do I notice?
What contrast is being made?
What does this reveal about the blessed life?
What does this reveal about God?
What is one faithful response?

Pause for reflection.

Common mistake:
One common mistake is asking, "What does this mean to me?" before asking, "What does this mean?" Personal application matters, but it should come after careful listening.

Reflection questions:
What does this passage reveal about God?
What is the difference between reading for information and reading for transformation?
Where do I need to slow down when I read Scripture?

Practice:
Read Psalm 1 three times this week. Write one observation, one question, and one response each time.

Closing prayer:
Lord, open my eyes to Your word and shape my life through it. Help me read not only to know more, but to love You, trust You, and obey You more faithfully. Amen.
