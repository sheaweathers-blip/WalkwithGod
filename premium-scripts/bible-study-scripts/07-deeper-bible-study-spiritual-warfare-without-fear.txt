Deeper Bible Study 7: Spiritual Warfare Without Fear
Length: About 40 minutes
Format: Teaching and prayer
Primary Scripture: Ephesians 6:10-18

Opening:
Welcome to Spiritual Warfare Without Fear.

Biblical spiritual warfare is not panic, superstition, or obsession with darkness. It is standing firm in the Lord through truth, righteousness, the gospel, faith, salvation, Scripture, and prayer.

Pray:
Lord, strengthen me to stand firm in truth, faith, and prayer. Amen.

Read Ephesians 6:10-18.

Teaching:
Paul begins, "Be strong in the Lord and in the strength of his might."

This is the foundation. Our strength is not self-generated. We stand in the Lord's strength.

Paul says to put on the whole armor of God. The armor is rooted in gospel realities, not human bravado.

The belt of truth:
Truth holds things together. Lies create confusion, shame, accusation, and fear. We stand by clinging to what God has revealed.

The breastplate of righteousness:
Righteousness guards the heart. This includes the righteousness we receive in Christ and the righteous life we are called to practice.

Shoes of gospel readiness:
The gospel gives steadiness. We stand ready because peace with God has been made through Christ.

The shield of faith:
Faith extinguishes flaming darts. Accusation, fear, temptation, and despair are met by trust in God.

The helmet of salvation:
Salvation guards the mind. We remember who has rescued us and whose we are.

The sword of the Spirit:
The Word of God is our given weapon. Jesus Himself answered temptation with Scripture.

Prayer:
Paul ends by calling believers to pray at all times in the Spirit. Prayer keeps us alert and dependent.

Reflection:
Where am I tempted to fight with fear, control, anger, or isolation?
Which piece of armor do I need to remember today?
What lie needs to be answered with truth?

Guided prayer:
Lord, fasten truth around me.
Guard my heart in righteousness.
Make me steady in the gospel of peace.
Lift faith over fear.
Guard my mind with salvation.
Teach me to use Your Word wisely.
Keep me prayerful and alert.

Practice:
Pray through each piece of armor and name one practical way to stand firm today.

Closing prayer:
Lord, strengthen me to stand firm in truth, faith, and prayer. Keep me from fear and help me resist evil with wisdom, humility, Scripture, and dependence on You. Amen.
