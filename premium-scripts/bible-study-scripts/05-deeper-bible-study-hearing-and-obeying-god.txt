Walk With God Abide
Deeper Bible Study 5: Hearing and Obeying God
Length: About 40 minutes
Format: Teaching and discernment
Primary Scripture: John 10:27

Opening:
Welcome to Hearing and Obeying God.

Many believers long to hear God's voice, especially when facing decisions. Scripture teaches us to listen through God's Word, the Holy Spirit, wisdom, counsel, fruit, and obedience.

Pray:
Good Shepherd, help me hear Your voice and follow with courage. Amen.

Read John 10:27.

Teaching:
Jesus says, "My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me."

Notice the order: hearing, being known, and following. Biblical listening is relational and obedient. We do not seek God's voice as a way to control the future. We listen because we belong to the Shepherd.

First: God's voice will not contradict Scripture.
The Holy Spirit does not lead us away from the Word He inspired. If an impression asks us to sin, manipulate, ignore wisdom, or reject Christlike love, it is not God's leading.

Second: listening is formed by relationship.
We learn God's ways over time through Scripture, prayer, worship, repentance, and obedience. Familiarity grows through walking with Him.

Third: wisdom often comes through counsel and fruit.
Proverbs teaches the value of wise counsel. Galatians teaches the fruit of the Spirit. A decision may sound spiritual, but if it produces pride, deception, isolation, or harm, we should slow down.

Fourth: obedience clarifies what we already know.
Sometimes we ask God for new direction while ignoring the clear direction He has already given: forgive, tell the truth, pray, serve, repent, love neighbor, seek wisdom, flee temptation.

Discernment practice:
Write one decision you are praying over.

Ask:
What does Scripture clearly say?
What wisdom have trusted believers offered?
What fruit might this decision produce?
Am I acting from love, faith, fear, pride, pressure, or impulse?
What is one faithful step I already know to take?

Reflection questions:
What has God already made clear that I have not acted on?
What voices compete with His voice?
Where do I need counsel rather than isolation?

Practice:
Test one decision through Scripture, wisdom, counsel, and fruit. Do not rush. Ask God for courage to obey the faithful next step.

Closing prayer:
Good Shepherd, help me hear Your voice and follow with courage. Guard me from fear, confusion, pride, and impulse. Make me attentive to Your Word and faithful in obedience. Amen.
