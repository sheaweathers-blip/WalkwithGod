Deeper Bible Study 3: Covenant and Promise
Length: About 35 minutes
Format: Teaching and worksheet
Primary Scripture: Genesis 12:1-3

Opening:
Welcome to Covenant and Promise.

The Bible's story moves through covenants. A covenant is more than a contract. It is a committed relationship shaped by promise, responsibility, faithfulness, and love.

Pray:
Faithful God, help me trust Your promises and live as one who belongs to You. Amen.

Read Genesis 12:1-3.

Teaching:
In Genesis 12, God calls Abram to go from his country, his kindred, and his father's house to the land God would show him. God promises to make him a great nation, bless him, make his name great, and bless all the families of the earth through him.

This moment is foundational. After Genesis 3 through 11 shows sin spreading through humanity, Genesis 12 shows God beginning a rescue plan through promise.

Covenant is relationship with promise.
God initiates. Abram does not design the plan. God calls, God promises, God leads.

Covenant includes responsibility.
Abram is called to go. Faith is not only agreement in the mind; it becomes trust expressed through obedience.

Covenant carries blessing outward.
God blesses Abram, but not for Abram alone. Through him, all families of the earth will be blessed. Biblical blessing is meant to move outward.

Covenant reveals God's faithfulness.
Abram and his descendants are often inconsistent. God remains faithful. The covenant story teaches us to look at God's character more than human performance.

Fulfillment in Christ:
Read Galatians 3:13-14.

Paul connects the blessing of Abraham to Jesus Christ. Jesus redeems us so that the blessing promised to Abraham might come to the nations. This means Genesis 12 is not just ancient family history. It is part of the gospel story.

Worksheet reflection:
Write the promises in Genesis 12:1-3.
Write the responsibilities or commands.
Write who receives blessing.
Write how Galatians 3:13-14 carries the promise forward.

Reflection questions:
What promises of God do I need to remember?
What responsibilities come with belonging to Him?
Where might God be blessing me so that others are blessed through me?

Practice:
Compare Genesis 12:1-3 and Galatians 3:13-14. Write three things that carry forward.

Closing prayer:
Faithful God, help me trust Your promises and live as one who belongs to You. Make my life a place where Your blessing moves outward in humility, obedience, and love. Amen.
