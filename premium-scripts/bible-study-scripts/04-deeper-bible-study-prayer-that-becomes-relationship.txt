Deeper Bible Study 4: Prayer That Becomes Relationship
Length: About 35 minutes
Format: Teaching and guided practice
Primary Scripture: Matthew 6:9-13

Opening:
Welcome to Prayer That Becomes Relationship.

Prayer is not only asking God for things. Prayer is relationship with the Father through worship, dependence, confession, request, surrender, and trust.

Pray:
Father, teach me to pray with trust, honesty, and surrender. Amen.

Read Matthew 6:9-13.

Teaching:
Jesus begins, "Our Father in heaven."

Prayer begins with relationship. We do not come first as performers, employees, or strangers. We come to the Father. This does not make prayer casual or careless, because He is also in heaven, holy and sovereign. Prayer holds nearness and reverence together.

"Hallowed be your name."
Prayer reorders worship. We begin by honoring God's name, not by centering our anxiety.

"Your kingdom come, your will be done."
Prayer reorders desire. We ask not only for our preferred outcomes, but for God's rule, justice, mercy, holiness, and purpose to become visible.

"Give us this day our daily bread."
Prayer teaches dependence. We bring real needs to God. Daily bread includes provision, strength, wisdom, and what is needed for faithful life today.

"Forgive us our debts."
Prayer includes confession. We do not hide from God. We bring sin into the light and receive mercy.

"As we also have forgiven our debtors."
Prayer shapes relationships. Receiving mercy makes us people who practice mercy.

"Lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil."
Prayer acknowledges spiritual need. We need guidance, protection, and deliverance.

Guided practice:
Pray the Lord's Prayer slowly.

After "Our Father," pause and name what it means to be loved by God.
After "Your kingdom come," name one place you want God's will more than your control.
After "daily bread," name one real need.
After "forgive us," confess honestly.
After "deliver us," ask for protection and wisdom.

Reflection questions:
Which part of the Lord's Prayer do I rush past?
How can prayer become more than asking for outcomes?
What would change if I began prayer with God's holiness and fatherly care?

Practice:
Pray the Lord's Prayer once a day this week, pausing after every phrase to personalize it.

Closing prayer:
Father, teach me to pray with trust, honesty, and surrender. Reorder my desires around Your kingdom and help prayer become daily relationship with You. Amen.
