Abide Breath Prayer: Week One Rollout Checklist

Purpose
Use this checklist to prepare the first seven Abide breath prayer videos before they are opened beyond admin preview.

App Setup
[ ] Review all seven videos inside the Abide Breath Prayer: Week One section while signed in as admin.
[ ] Mark each video watched after checking the title, Scripture, description, and YouTube playback.
[ ] Replace "Finished video" labels with exact minute-and-second durations after final YouTube details are confirmed.
[ ] Decide whether regular testers should see only the titles or receive access to selected Week One videos.
[ ] When ready, update the locked message from "coming later" to the access wording you want users to see.

YouTube Setup
[ ] Make sure each video title starts with "Abide Breath Prayer" or "Walk With God Breath Prayer."
[ ] Add the website link to every video description: https://walk-with-god.org
[ ] Add a short disclaimer: breathe naturally, stop if uncomfortable, and seek medical advice when needed.
[ ] Add a consistent thumbnail style using the Walk With God logo.
[ ] Group the first seven videos into a YouTube playlist named "Abide Breath Prayer: Week One."

Content Review
[ ] Confirm each session is grounded in its Scripture.
[ ] Confirm every phrase points users toward God, prayer, peace, and dependence on Him.
[ ] Keep language gentle and simple, especially for anxious or weary users.
[ ] Avoid making health claims. Present breath prayer as a spiritual practice with calming support, not medical treatment.

Opening Beyond Admin
[ ] Test on phone and laptop while signed in as admin.
[ ] Test on a non-admin account to confirm videos are still hidden until you choose to open them.
[ ] Choose the first small tester group for Abide access.
[ ] Ask testers whether the videos feel peaceful, clear, biblically grounded, and easy to follow.
