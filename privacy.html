Walk With God

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Overview

Walk With God helps users build daily faith habits through Scripture focuses, reminders, notes, prayer requests, and community encouragement. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We may collect account information such as name, email address, password credentials, reminder preferences, phone number if text reminders are selected, saved progress, private notes, community posts, prayer requests, feedback, and basic technical information needed to operate the app.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide accounts, save progress, send selected reminders, display community features, respond to feedback, improve the app, and support user accounts.

SMS And Email Reminders

Text reminders are optional. If a user chooses text reminders and provides a phone number, Walk With God may send reminder text messages based on that user's reminder settings. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Users can reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers to operate the app, such as hosting, email, text messaging, and notification providers. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services.

Private Notes

Private notes are intended for the user's own reflection. Admin support tools may show that notes exist for support metrics, but private note content is not intended to be displayed to admins.

Contact

For privacy questions or account help, contact walkedwithgodtoday@gmail.com.
