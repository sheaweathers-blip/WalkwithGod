# Walk With God

Walk With God is a Christ-centered discipleship platform designed to help people take one intentional step with God each day through Scripture, prayer, reflection, active time with God, community, notes, and reminders.

The product north star is:

Take your next step with God today.

## Start Here

Read the [Walk With God Design Bible](WALK_WITH_GOD_DESIGN_BIBLE.md) before making major product, content, design, or technical decisions.

That document defines:

- Mission and product principles
- The seven-step Walk With God Path
- Theology and safety boundaries
- Language to use and avoid
- Community and prayer standards
- Premium content standards
- Living Stones future-phase guidance
- The decision filter for new features

## Current App

The current app includes:

- Account creation and login
- Daily focus journeys
- Full Scripture passage display and read-aloud support
- 15-20 minute active time with God
- Application questions and deeds of the day
- Private notes and saved note library
- Prayer Library
- Community posts, comments, reactions, and prayer requests
- Reminder settings for app notification, text, and email infrastructure
- Admin support, feedback, reporting, and moderation
- Premium previews for breathwork, faith-led yoga, walking sessions, and Bible study
