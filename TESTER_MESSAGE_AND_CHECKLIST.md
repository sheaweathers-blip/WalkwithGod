# Tester Message For Walk With God

Hi! I would love your help testing my new app, Walk With God.

Please use this link:

https://walk-with-god.org

Create a free account, explore the app, and send feedback inside the app using the Feedback section. Premium features are only previews right now, so nothing should ask you to pay.

Reminder note: email and text reminders are not active for testing yet. Please only test app/browser notifications for reminders if your device supports them.

## What To Test

- Create a free account.
- Log out and log back in.
- Use the Start Here checklist.
- Choose a focus from the Focus Library.
- Open a day and read the Scripture passage.
- Try the Read Passage Aloud button.
- Save a private note.
- Mark one day complete.
- Open your private notes library.
- Try the Community section.
- Try Prayer Requests.
- Review Reminder Settings and test app notifications if your device supports them.
- Open the Premium preview sections:
  - Breathwork Prayer
  - Faith-Led Yoga
  - Bible Study
  - Walking Sessions
- Use the app on your phone if you can.
- Try adding the app to your phone home screen if your browser offers it.

## Feedback I Need Most

- Was it easy to create an account?
- Did you know what to do first?
- Did anything not work?
- Did anything feel confusing or crowded?
- Did the daily focus feel spiritually helpful?
- Did the active time with God idea make sense?
- Would you use the notes, community, or prayer request features?
- Which premium preview sounds most useful?
- What would make you come back tomorrow?

## Helpful Details To Include

When you send feedback, please include:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened
- Whether you were on phone, tablet, or computer
- Browser if you know it, such as Chrome, Safari, or Edge
- Screenshot if something looks wrong

Thank you for helping me make this better.
