SMS Consent

Last updated: June 8, 2026

How Users Opt In

Users opt in to Walk With God text reminders by creating an account, opening Reminder Settings, choosing Text message as a reminder method, entering a phone number, and checking the SMS consent box before saving reminder settings.

What Messages Are Sent

Walk With God sends faith-based reminder text messages connected to the user's daily reminder settings. Messages may remind users to spend time with God, continue a daily focus, or return to prayer and Scripture.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on the user's reminder settings. A typical user may receive up to one reminder per day when text reminders are enabled.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply depending on the user's mobile carrier and plan.

Opt Out And Help

Users can reply STOP to opt out of text reminders. Users can reply HELP for help. Users may also contact walkedwithgodtoday@gmail.com for support.

Privacy

Phone numbers are used to send selected text reminders and related support. Walk With God does not sell personal information. See our Privacy Policy for more information.
