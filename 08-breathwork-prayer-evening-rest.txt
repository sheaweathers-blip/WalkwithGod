Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Evening Rest
Category: Breathwork Prayer
Estimated Length: 6-8 minutes
Scripture: Psalm 4:8, "In peace I will both lie down and sleep; for you alone, O Lord, make me dwell in safety."

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's breathwork prayer is called Evening Rest.

This practice is meant for the end of the day, or any time you need to slow down and place the day in God's hands.

Find a restful position. You may sit with support or lie down. Let your body be comfortable. Let your eyes close if that feels safe.

If any breathing pattern feels uncomfortable, release the pattern and breathe normally.

Our Scripture today is Psalm 4:8:
"In peace I will both lie down and sleep; for you alone, O Lord, make me dwell in safety."

Take a slow breath in.
And gently breathe out.

Again, breathe in.
And breathe out.

Let your exhale be unhurried.

Inhale:
"Lord, You are with me."

Exhale:
"I place this day in Your hands."

Inhale:
"Lord, You are with me."

Exhale:
"I place this day in Your hands."

Now quietly remember one gift from today.
Something small is enough.
A kindness.
A breath.
A meal.
A moment of strength.
A word of comfort.

Thank God for it.

Pause.

Inhale:
"Thank You for today."

Exhale:
"Your mercy carried me."

Inhale:
"Thank You for today."

Exhale:
"Your mercy carried me."

Now bring before God anything unfinished.
The task not done.
The conversation unresolved.
The worry still present.
The thing you wish had gone differently.

You do not have to hold it through the night.

Inhale:
"You are faithful."

Exhale:
"I release what is unfinished."

Inhale:
"You are faithful."

Exhale:
"I release what is unfinished."

If there is anything to confess, you can bring it simply.
No hiding.
No fear.
God is merciful.

Pause.

Inhale:
"Have mercy, Lord."

Exhale:
"Lead me again tomorrow."

Inhale:
"Have mercy, Lord."

Exhale:
"Lead me again tomorrow."

Now take three quiet breaths.

Pause.

Lord, thank You for carrying me through this day. Thank You for what was good, and thank You for Your mercy over what was hard, unfinished, or broken. I place my body, mind, home, loved ones, and tomorrow into Your hands.

Let me rest in Your care.
Let me sleep in Your peace.
Let me wake ready to walk with You again.

Amen.
