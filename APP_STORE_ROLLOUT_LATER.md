# App Store Rollout Topic For Later

Saved question:

> How do I get Walk With God as an app in the Google Play Store or Apple App Store?

Recommended order:

1. Keep sharing the installable web app now.
2. Prepare Google Play first because it is usually easier than Apple.
3. Create a Google Play Console developer account.
4. Package the existing Walk With God web app as an Android app, likely with a Trusted Web Activity.
5. Submit for Google Play testing and review.
6. After Google Play is working, prepare the Apple version using TestFlight and App Store Connect.

Useful notes:

- Google Play Console has a one-time developer registration fee.
- Apple Developer Program has an annual membership fee.
- Apple may reject apps that feel like only a website wrapper, so the iPhone version should feel app-like and clearly useful.
- Walk With God already has helpful app-like features: accounts, daily progress, reminders, community, notes, prayer requests, premium previews, and admin tools.
