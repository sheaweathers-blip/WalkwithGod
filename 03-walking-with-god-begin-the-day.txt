Walk With God Abide Script
Title: Begin the Day With God
Category: Walking With God
Estimated Length: 12-15 minutes
Scripture: Psalm 143:8

Welcome to Walk With God.

Today's guided walk is called Begin the Day With God.

Before you begin, choose a safe place to walk. Stay aware of your surroundings. Walk at a comfortable pace.

Our Scripture is Psalm 143:8:
"Let the morning bring me word of your unfailing love, for I have put my trust in you. Show me the way I should go, for to you I entrust my life."

Begin walking slowly.

Notice your first few steps.
Notice your breath.
Notice the ground beneath you.

You are not just exercising.
You are making space to walk with God.

As you walk, pray:
"Lord, I give You this day."

Take a few moments to name the day before God.
What is ahead of you?
What responsibilities are waiting?
What conversations, decisions, or worries are already on your mind?

You can speak them quietly to God now.

Pause.

Now return to the verse:
"Show me the way I should go."

As you continue walking, ask God:
"What is one faithful step for me today?"

Not the whole plan.
Not every answer.
Just one faithful step.

Maybe it is patience.
Maybe it is courage.
Maybe it is forgiveness.
Maybe it is rest.
Maybe it is obedience in something small.

Walk in silence for a moment and listen.

Pause.

Now think of one person you will encounter or think about today.
Pray for them as you walk.

Ask God to bless them.
Ask God to help you love them well.
Ask God to make you attentive and kind.

Pause.

Now shift your attention to gratitude.

With each few steps, name one gift:
Breath.
Strength.
Mercy.
Shelter.
Forgiveness.
Another day.
God's presence.

Let gratitude become part of your pace.

As we begin to close, slow your steps.

Pray this with me:

Lord, let this day begin with You.
Remind me of Your unfailing love.
Teach me to trust You with what I cannot control.
Show me the way I should go.
Help my words, choices, body, and attention honor You today.

Walk with me, Lord.

Amen.
