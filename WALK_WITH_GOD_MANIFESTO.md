# The Walk With God Manifesto

This is who we are.

## We Believe

We believe that God still walks with His people.

We believe He still speaks through His Word.

We believe prayer still changes us.

We believe ordinary moments become holy when surrendered to Him.

We believe beauty can point beyond itself to the Creator.

We believe one faithful step is more important than a thousand hurried ones.

We believe discipleship happens in daily rhythms, not only occasional events.

We believe no one walks alone.

We believe every believer is still being shaped by the Master Builder.

We believe Jesus Christ is the Cornerstone upon which everything else must stand.

## We Will

Create with excellence.

Teach Scripture faithfully.

Serve with humility.

Welcome questions.

Celebrate growth.

Walk beside people through every season.

Honor God above recognition.

Remember that this ministry belongs to Him.

## We Will Never

Use fear to motivate.

Use guilt to manipulate.

Use Scripture carelessly.

Promise what God has not promised.

Replace relationship with routine.

Forget that behind every screen is a person loved by God.

## Our Prayer

May every page, every prayer, every pendant, every guided walk, every conversation, every breath, every line of code, and every decision point to Jesus.

Before we design, we pray.

Before we publish, we pray.

Before we launch, we pray.

After every answered prayer, we give thanks.

## Our Guiding Question

Will this help someone take one more step with God today?

## Book Of Remembrance Prayer

May every new idea be measured not by how impressive it is, but by whether it helps people know Jesus more deeply and walk with Him more faithfully.
