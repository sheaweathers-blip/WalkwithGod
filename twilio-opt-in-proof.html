Business And Use Case

Walk With God offers optional daily faith reminder text messages for users who create a free account and intentionally choose text reminders in their profile reminder settings.

Text messages may include daily Scripture focus reminders, prayer/reflection prompts, and account-related reminder updates. Users may receive up to 1 message per day based on their saved reminder settings.

How Users Opt In

  1. User creates a free Walk With God account or logs in.
  2. User opens Account, then Reminder Settings.
  3. User selects Text message as a reminder method.
  4. User enters their own mobile phone number.
  5. User checks the SMS consent checkbox, which is not required to create an account or use the free app.
  6. User saves the reminder settings.

Consent Checkbox Text

By checking this box, I agree to receive recurring text message reminders from Walk With God at the phone number I provide. These messages may include daily Scripture focus reminders, prayer/reflection prompts, and account-related reminder updates. Message frequency may vary, up to 1 message per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe and HELP for help. Consent is not required to create an account or use the free app. See the SMS Consent, Privacy Policy, and Terms.
