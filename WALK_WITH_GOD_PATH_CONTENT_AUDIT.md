# Walk With God Path Content Audit

Date: July 9, 2026

## Core Framework

The app now uses the Walk With God Path as the discipleship rhythm behind the daily experience:

1. Pause - Psalm 46:10 - Jesus withdrew to quiet places (Luke 5:16).
2. Pray - Mark 1:35 - Jesus prayed to the Father (Mark 1:35).
3. Listen - John 5:19 - Jesus lived in dependence on the Father's will (John 5:19).
4. Walk - Luke 24:15 - Jesus walked with people and taught along the way (Luke 24:15).
5. Live - John 13:14-15 - Jesus embodied humble love and service (John 13:14-15).
6. Remember - Luke 22:19 - Jesus gave His people a way to remember Him (Luke 22:19).
7. Share - Matthew 28:19-20 - Jesus sent His followers to make disciples (Matthew 28:19-20).

This keeps the app from feeling like a checklist. The invitation is:

Take your next step with God today.

## How The Path Ties To Each Daily Focus

Each day now receives a Path step by looking first at the day's theme, title, reference, and summary.

Examples:

- Prayer, confession, repentance, or asking God for wisdom maps to Pray.
- Scripture, wisdom, truth, commands, prophetic voices, and discernment map to Listen.
- Body, health, wellness, creation, strength, and embodied worship map to Walk.
- Service, obedience, forgiveness, mercy, love, fruit, marriage, good works, and purpose map to Live.
- Covenant, redemption, gratitude, God's love, and mercy map to Remember.
- Witness, mission, good news, light, testimony, and community map to Share.
- Rest, stillness, peace, anxiety, fear, grief, hardship, and compassion map to Pause.

If a day does not clearly match one of those content cues, it falls back to the seven-step rhythm by day number. This keeps the Path connected to the verse/theme while still preserving a simple repeatable structure.

## Biblical Accuracy Notes

No major theological red flags stood out in the current focus library structure. The references are broadly appropriate to their themes and remain centered on Scripture.

Areas to keep especially clear:

- Manifestation in the Bible: Keep wording anchored in God revealing His presence, glory, love, character, promises, and kingdom. Avoid implying that users manifest outcomes through personal power.
- Self-Love and Confidence: Keep the tone rooted in being made in God's image, belonging to Christ, humility, stewardship, and love of neighbor. Avoid drifting into self-worship or self-exaltation.
- Health and Wellness: Keep body care framed as gratitude, stewardship, rest, and worship, not body perfection or fitness pressure.
- Divorce and Healing: Keep language compassionate and careful. Avoid one-size-fits-all advice. Encourage safety, wise counsel, prayer, repentance where needed, and healing.
- Angels in the Bible: Keep the focus on God, not fascination with angels. Revelation 22:8-9 is a strong closing anchor because worship belongs to God alone.
- Purpose: Keep purpose rooted in God's glory, Christlike formation, gifts, service, and obedience, not achievement or personal platform.

## Content Quality Notes

The focus library is strong, but the next best refinement would be making every theme feel even more custom in the application questions and deeds.

Already strong:

- Self-Love and Confidence has custom follow-ups.
- How God Loves You has custom follow-ups.
- Purpose has custom follow-ups.
- Manifestation in the Bible has custom follow-ups.
- Health and Wellness in the Bible has custom follow-ups.
- The Holy Spirit has custom follow-ups.
- Prayer and Listening has custom follow-ups.
- Marriage has custom follow-ups.
- Divorce and Healing has custom follow-ups.
- Deed of the day is already tied to many Scripture keywords and rotates across themes.

Recommended future refinement:

- Continue adding custom follow-ups for the remaining themes where a distinct tone would help, especially The Ten Commandments, Women of the Bible, Prophets of the Bible, Angels in the Bible, and Serving and Mission.
- Review long-term whether each theme should have a written pastoral note or short intro that explains how to approach sensitive material.

## UX Recommendations

1. Keep "Take your next step with God today" as the primary app invitation.
2. Use "step" language more often than "complete" language where possible.
3. Keep completion celebration, but make it feel like gratitude and remembrance rather than achievement.
4. Consider adding a "What was your next step today?" prompt to check-ins.
5. In premium, organize future content around the Path:
   - Pause: Breathwork prayer
   - Pray: Prayer sessions and guided journaling
   - Listen: Deeper Bible studies
   - Walk: Guided prayer walks
   - Live: Faith-led movement and application challenges
   - Remember: Reflection library, saved notes, answered prayers
   - Share: Community, prayer, testimony, classes
