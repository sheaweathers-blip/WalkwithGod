Why We Walk

Helping people take one more step with God - one day, one prayer, one walk at a time.

Before there was a website, an app, handcrafted reminders, or guided walks, there was simply a desire.

A desire to know God more deeply. A desire to make space for Him in everyday life. A desire to encourage others to do the same.

Walk With God was born from the belief that our relationship with God is not built only in extraordinary moments. It is shaped in the ordinary rhythms of daily life.

Every part of this ministry exists for one purpose: to help people take one more step with God today.

Our Heart

God is still inviting people to walk with Him.

Not only in church. Not only on Sundays. But in kitchens, on hiking trails, during morning coffee, on difficult days, while driving to work, in seasons of joy, and in moments of uncertainty.

Walking with God is not another task to complete. It is a relationship to cultivate.

That is why everything we create - from the app to handcrafted collections, guided walks, Scripture journeys, breath prayers, and community - is designed to gently help people build a daily rhythm of meeting with Jesus.

Meet A Fellow Walker

Hello, I am Shea.

I am not a pastor. I am not someone who has everything figured out. I am simply someone who loves Jesus and is learning, just like everyone else, what it means to walk faithfully with Him one day at a time.

Like many people, my journey has included questions, growth, waiting, joy, and learning to trust God more deeply through every season.

Over time, the Lord placed a desire on my heart to create something that would help others slow down, spend intentional time with Him, and discover that walking with God is not reserved for a few spiritual moments each week. It can become the rhythm of everyday life.

My hope is not that people would know my name. My hope is that they would know Jesus more deeply because something here encouraged them to spend time with Him.

The Walk With God Manifesto

We believe God still walks with His people.

We believe God still speaks through His Word.

We believe prayer still changes us.

We believe ordinary moments become holy when surrendered to Him.

We believe one faithful step is more important than a thousand hurried ones.

We believe Jesus Christ is the Cornerstone upon which everything else must stand.

Our Prayer

May everything point to Jesus.

If something you find here encourages you, may it lead you to spend more time with Jesus.

If something here comforts you, may you remember that He is near.

If something here challenges you, may it draw you closer to His Word.

If something here reminds you of God's faithfulness, may all glory be given to Him.
